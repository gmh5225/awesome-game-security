# Linux anti-cheat compatibility — feasibility study

Independent systems-security research by [IZxMD](https://github.com/IZxMD).

This repository documents a **literature and architecture feasibility study**: whether a vendor-agnostic compatibility layer on Linux could host kernel anti-cheat components with **real security equivalence**, without virtualization as the primary path.

It is **not** a product, game, cheat, or bypass. There is **no implementation** of an anti-cheat compatibility layer here yet.

## Scope

- What kernel anti-cheats actually require from a Windows kernel (public sources only)
- What Linux can and cannot provide (LSM, modules, signing, isolation, ABI)
- Non-virtualized design options and hard falsification
- A dossier and knowledge graph meant as a later engineering spec, not as shipping code

## Out of scope

- Reverse-engineering closed anti-cheat binaries
- Bypass, spoof, or hide techniques
- Game-specific hacks (titles are examples only)

## Status

Phase: environment and corpus setup. Papers, notes, and the feasibility dossier will land here as the study proceeds.

## Contact

GitHub: [IZxMD](https://github.com/IZxMD)
