# GreyM

C/C++ project focused on PE (Portable Executable), listed under Anti Cheat → Binary Packer with a `[PE]` tag. Centers on debugging workflows for packed PE clients—aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers studying binary packers rather than shipping as an AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/greyb1t__GreyM.md)

Useful as a PE packing and debugging reference alongside other PE packers—not a full unpacker or commercial protector.
