# android_kernel_oneplus_sm7250-WKSU

Custom Android kernel source for OnePlus Nord (Qualcomm SM7250) with [[kernelsu]] patches for systemless root. Based on the SM7250 BSP; aimed at kernel developers and custom ROM builders who need KernelSU on OnePlus Nord. Listed under Cheat / Android Kernel Source as KernelSU for Oneplus. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/psavarmattas__android_kernel_oneplus_sm7250-WKSU.md)
