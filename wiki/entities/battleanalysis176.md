# BattleAnalysis176

**BattleAnalysis176** (Bratah123/BattleAnalysis176) is a lightweight **C++ console** utility that estimates **MapleStory v176** farming performance from live client memory. It reads in-game values through **pointers and offsets**, then projects **mesos** and **NX** gains over hourly intervals. The design centers on memory-reading logic rather than a graphical interface, targeting private-server operators and reverse engineers who want quick runtime economy metrics. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Bratah123__BattleAnalysis176.md)
