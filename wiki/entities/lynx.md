# Lynx

Rooted Android **performance and system tuning** companion: a **Kotlin** client works with a packaged **native daemon** and shell automation to read/write **kernel knobs**, CPU/GPU scheduling, vendor graphics stacks (including MediaTek **GED** and **FPSGO**), thermal policy, block I/O schedulers, and **TCP sysctl** settings for lower network latency. It exposes dashboards and telemetry for CPU clusters, GPU, battery, memory, and thermal state, plus a floating game HUD with overlays, recording helpers, and combo/automation features, with some coverage of common game engines and libraries. It targets advanced users who accept invasive kernel- and vendor-level changes. The catalog entry describes a **Magisk module** oriented at **Snapdragon** gaming performance and battery tuning. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Zy0x__Lynx.md)
