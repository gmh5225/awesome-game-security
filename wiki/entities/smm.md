# smm (ekknod)

**smm** is an **SMM cheat** project — offensive research targeting **System Management Mode** (Ring -2), the firmware-resident execution environment below the OS and typical hypervisor layers. It is written in **C**, **C/C++**, and centers on **driver development**, **graphics**, and **networking** for game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying cheat / EFI driver techniques.
