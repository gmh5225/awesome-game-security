# JinYongLegend

"群侠传，启动！" (*Wuxia, Start!*) — a completed Jin Yong–style wuxia RPG framework with full modding support and multi-platform releases. Unity-based game and toolchain for long-form single-player RPG content plus community modding. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/jynew__jynew.md)

Useful for game developers and modders studying Chinese wuxia RPG frameworks and mod pipelines—not a cheat or anti-cheat artifact.
