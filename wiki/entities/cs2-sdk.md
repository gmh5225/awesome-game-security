# cs2-sdk

C/C++ **Counter-Strike 2** SDK (gmh5225) focused on **SDK generation** for the **Source 2** engine—headers and scaffolding for entity classes, engine interfaces, and cheat-oriented type systems. Emphasis areas include **driver development**, **rendering**, and **networking**. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / game:cs2 lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__cs2_sdk.md)

Sits beside generated SDK tooling such as [[source2gen]] and [[source2sdk]], maintained offset feeds such as [[cs2-offsets]], and research collections such as [[cs2-things]] as a CS2 layout and interface artifact.
