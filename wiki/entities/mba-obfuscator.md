# MBA-Obfuscator

Non-linear Mixed Boolean-Arithmetic (MBA) expression obfuscator. Implementation lives under `mba_obfuscator/`; generated samples under `samples/`. Useful for game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the Cheat Mixed boolean-arithmetic lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/nhpcc502__MBA-Obfuscator.md)

Generation/obfuscation-side counterpart to MBA sample generators such as [[mutaben]] and simplifiers such as [[cobra]].
