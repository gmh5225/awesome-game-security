# mhynot2

Research-oriented tool to **bypass miHoYo / Genshin Impact cheat-prevention** — framed by the author as experimental study of whether requiring a kernel driver (`mhyprot2.sys`) to play a mostly single-player title is worse than the cheating it blocks. Not marketed as a cheat product; useful for game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / game:genshin impact lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/khang06__mhynot2.md)

Contrasts with [[evil-mhyprot-cli]], which abuses the signed AC driver as a [[byovd]] primitive rather than avoiding its load requirement.
