# OpenRCT2

Open-source C++ reimplementation of **RollerCoaster Tycoon 2** focused on modern systems. Recreates park-simulation gameplay while adding cooperative multiplayer, improved interface and AI behavior, expanded editing tools, and higher engine limits. **CMake**-based builds with broad platform support; exposes scripting and plugin integration points for extensibility. Intended for players and developers who want a maintainable simulation engine for amusement-park game technology—not a cheat or anti-cheat artifact. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/OpenRCT2__OpenRCT2.md)

Distinct from general engine catalogs such as [[open-source-engines]]; this is the **RCT2 reimplementation** lane for studying classic-title engine recreation, modding surfaces, and simulation internals beside remakes such as [[openclaw]], [[xray-16]], and [[devilution]].
