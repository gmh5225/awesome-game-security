# ImGui-Spoofer-Leaked

**ImGui-Spoofer-Leaked** is a leaked Windows **hardware identity (HWID) spoofer** with an **ImGui** graphical interface. Implemented primarily in C++, it bundles **loader and mapper** components for kernel-level interaction alongside WinAPI-based system routines. The codebase includes **anti-analysis** behavior such as debugger and process checks, indicating design for adversarial environments. Its primary research value is understanding how ban-evasion-style spoofer tooling is structured in game-cheating ecosystems. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Veuqx0__ImGui-Spoofer-Leaked.md)

Sits in the `Cheat > HWID` lane beside ImGui-loader HWID spoofer bases such as [[hwid-spoofer-ud-fortnite-warzone-apex-rust-escape-from-tarkov-and-all-eac-be-games-imgui-loader-base]] and [[full-hwid-spoofer-v6]], kernel-mode EAC/BE samples such as [[hwid-spoofer-eac-be]] and [[easy-hwid-spoofer]], and Detection:HWID counterparts such as [[hwid-checker-mg]].
