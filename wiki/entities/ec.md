# EC (ekknod)

**EC** is an offensive game-security research project for **CS:GO**, written in **C/C++** and centered on **kernel-level work**, **driver development**, and **OpenGL** rendering paths for researchers studying cheat / game:csgo techniques. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ekknod__EC.md)

The curated README entry points to the related [[ec-pro-lan]] variant (FACEIT AC exploration with constrained lab hardware). EC sits in ekknod's CS:GO stack beside [[nv-v2]], [[csf]], and [[g37obs]].
