# jvm-inspector

PowerShell **Minecraft Java screenshare forensics** toolkit for staff reviewing a suspect PC during consensual PC checks. Combines a **live JVM audit** (loaded `javaagent` JARs, suspicious launch flags, injection-related JVM options) with **disk sweeps** for renamed or repacked JARs and signature matches against known cheat-client patterns. Intended for human-review evidence collection alongside static JAR analyzers such as [[jaranalyzer]] and workflow platforms such as [[error-pc-check]]. (source: wiki/sources/README-categories.md)
