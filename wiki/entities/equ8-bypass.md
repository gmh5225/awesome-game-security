# equ8_bypass

Simple **EQU8 user-mode bypass** that locates the anti-cheat driver path via the registry and closes the IOCTL handle to disrupt usermode↔driver communication. Listed under cheat / explore anticheat system:equ8; aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive bypass techniques against EQU8-protected titles.
