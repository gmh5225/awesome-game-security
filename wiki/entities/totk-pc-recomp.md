# totk-pc-recomp

Static recompilation of *The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom* to native **Windows x86-64** with a **Vulkan** renderer. Users supply their own game assets; the repository ships no copyrighted content. Listed in the Cheat / Source lane beside other Switch AArch64 lift-and-port projects. (source: wiki/sources/README-categories.md)

## Research notes

Treat lifted guest code separately from reimplemented Horizon/NVN-style host services—same [[static-runtime-evidence]] discipline as [[smo-recomp]] and [[letsgo-recomp]] before citing renderer or gameplay fidelity.
