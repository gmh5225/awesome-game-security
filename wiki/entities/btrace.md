# btrace

Android **app dynamic behavior tracking** toolkit built on **eBPF** (C/C++ and Go). Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / RE-tools lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/null-luo__btrace.md)

Sits beside Android eBPF packet/TLS tooling such as [[peetch]] and non-attach process watching such as [[pwatch]].
