# wizard101-spoofer

**HWID (Hardware Identification) spoofer** for **Wizard101** that modifies system identifiers to evade hardware bans — disk serial numbers, MAC addresses, motherboard UUIDs, and other hardware fingerprints the title's ban system uses. Aimed at game-security researchers studying HWID-based ban systems and spoofing techniques. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__wizard101-spoofer.md)

Sits alongside general Windows HWID spoofers such as [[hwidspoofer]], title-targeted samples such as [[hwid-spoofer-for-fortnite-and-valorant]], and Detection:HWID counterparts such as [[hwid-checker-mg]].
