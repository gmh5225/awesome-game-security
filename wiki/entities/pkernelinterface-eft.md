# pKernelInterface-EFT

C++ **external Escape from Tarkov reversal framework** from **Nou4r** with cheat-oriented functionality. Integrates **kernel-assisted memory interaction**, entity processing, and gameplay modules such as ESP and aiming logic. Includes an **ImGui rendering stack** for overlay output and tooling interfaces. Main use case is practical reverse engineering and prototyping for game security research on protected titles. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Nou4r__pKernelInterface-EFT.md)

Sits in the driver-backed external EFT lane beside kernel externals such as [[eft-external]] and [[eft-streamed-cheat]], DMA radars such as [[meatyeftrelease]] and [[eft-dma-radar-1]], OOP cheat bases such as [[simple-eft-base]], and hybrid/QEMU frameworks such as [[escape-from-tuxkov]].
