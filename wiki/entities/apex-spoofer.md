# Apex-Spoofer

**Apex-Spoofer** is a gmh5225 repo in the `Cheat > HWID` lane centered on **Apex Legends** hardware-ID (HWID) spoofing. It focuses on anti-cheat research, kernel-level work, and modding for game-security researchers studying offensive HWID-ban evasion on [[easy-anti-cheat]]–protected clients. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__Apex-Spoofer.md)
