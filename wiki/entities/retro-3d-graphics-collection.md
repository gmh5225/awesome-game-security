# Retro3DGraphicsCollection

Curated index of commercially usable retro 3D assets in a PS1 style. Delivered as a README-centered catalog linking models, textures, related packs, and shader resources under permissive CC0-style usage terms. Content is grouped by themes such as nature, contemporary, fantasy, and UI-oriented assets—aimed at indie developers and prototyping teams that need retro visual content quickly. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Miziziziz__Retro3DGraphicsCollection.md)

Sits in the README **Game Assets** lane beside curated asset indexes such as [[awesome-unity]] and AI/YAML→3D pipelines such as [[3d-asset-factory]]—a permissive retro art source for engine prototyping and stylized rendering experiments, not a cheat or anti-cheat artifact.

## Security-relevant surfaces

- **Shader references** — linked PS1-style shader resources sit upstream of draw-call and material pipelines studied in [[overviews/graphics-api]].
- **Permissive asset packs** — CC0-style terms lower friction for building offline security-research sandboxes and stylized test scenes without proprietary art constraints.
