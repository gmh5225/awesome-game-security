# WzComparerR2

MapleStory online **WZ extractor** and viewer (gmh5225): C# and Lua codebase centered on rendering, graphics, and networking for browsing and exporting MapleStory client asset archives. README tag: Maplestory online Extractor. Listed for game security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in cheat / game:maplestory. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__WzComparerR2.md)

Complements WZ pack/unpack tooling such as [[maplestory-packer-modpacker]] on the client asset-pipeline side and private-server stacks such as [[mnwvs196]] and [[ms079]] that consume WZ bundles.
