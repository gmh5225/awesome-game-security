# GTAIV EFLC FusionFix

Comprehensive **ASI plugin** fix and enhancement mod for **GTA IV** and **Episodes from Liberty City** (Complete Edition). Patches rendering bugs, improves graphics quality, fixes gameplay issues, and adds configurable visual settings through ASI injection—covering graphics, FPS, QoL, scripting, and widescreen support. Complements RTX remaster tooling such as [[gta4-rtx]] on the legacy DirectX client. Useful for DirectX compatibility, graphics-mod, and Windows game-tooling research.
