# Shadow

Jailbreak detection bypass for modern iOS jailbreaks. Success is not guaranteed across all apps — the project documents ideas and techniques to try when a title’s client-side jailbreak checks block play on a rooted device. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/jjolano__shadow.md)

Mainly useful for game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / iOS jailbreak lane — the attacker-side counterpart to jailbreak probes in SDKs such as [[free-rasp-ios]] and [[trustdevice-ios]].
