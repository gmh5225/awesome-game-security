# disable-threat-tracing

C project in the Anti Cheat → ETW Testing lane for stress-testing / disabling threat-tracing telemetry. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers studying ETW Threat-Intelligence and related host telemetry surfaces. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/muturikaranja__disable-threat-tracing.md)

Complements ThreatIntel consumers such as [[tietwagent]] / [[threat-intelligence-consumer]], ETW TI blinders such as [[edrsandblast]], provider browsers such as [[etw-explorer]], and synthetic ETW generators such as [[bamboozledr]].
