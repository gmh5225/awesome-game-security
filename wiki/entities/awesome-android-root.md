# Awesome Android Root

Curated **awesome list** of Android root resources — frameworks, modules, guides, and references for gaining and maintaining elevated privileges on Android devices. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / Android root area. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/fynks__awesome-android-root.md)

Index hub rather than a single root binary; complements hands-on frameworks such as [[magisk]], [[kernelsu]], and APatch/KPM collections such as [[apatch-kpm]], plus curated ROM references such as [[android-rom-list]]. Notes that **Play Integrity** enforcement is stricter on Android 14/15 — relevant when pairing root-hide research with mobile anti-cheat integrity checks. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/fynks__awesome-android-root.md)
