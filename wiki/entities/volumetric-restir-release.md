# Volumetric ReSTIR Release

Research implementation of **spatiotemporal reservoir resampling** for fast volumetric rendering. Integrates a custom volumetric render pass into a **Falcor**-based C++ and **Slang** renderer, with scripts and scene pipelines to reproduce paper results. Targets real-time quality and performance on modern RTX-class hardware for graphics researchers and engine developers exploring advanced rendering techniques. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/DQLin__VolumetricReSTIRRelease.md)

Primary use case is volumetric rendering research—not anti-cheat or reverse engineering. Sits beside other ReSTIR study surfaces ([[strolle]], [[kajiya]]) as a Falcor/RTX volumetric sampling reference.
