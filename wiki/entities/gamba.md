# GAMBA

**GAMBA** (Denuvo Software Solutions) is a research tool for simplifying **Mixed Boolean-Arithmetic (MBA)** expressions. The Python-based simplifier handles **linear and nonlinear MBA** forms, includes a **bitwise expression factory**, and ships **experiment datasets** from related tools such as NeuReduce, QSynth, and Syntia. The repository also bundles the associated research paper and presentation slides. It targets obfuscation and deobfuscation researchers studying MBA-based code protection and automated expression simplification. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/DenuvoSoftwareSolutions__GAMBA.md)

Complements algebraic MBA simplifiers such as [[goomba]], [[cobra]], and [[mbased]], synthesis-oriented tools such as [[promba]] and [[qsynthesis]], and the broader [[mixed-boolean-arithmetic]] recovery landscape. Also related to other Denuvo research tooling such as [[onlooker]].
