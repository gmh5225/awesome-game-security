# awesome-ida-x64-olly-plugin

Curated **awesome list** of plugins and scripts for major reverse-engineering tools — **IDA Pro**, **Ghidra**, **x64dbg**, **OllyDbg**, and **GDB**. Entries span patching, binary diffing, deobfuscation, emulation, code coverage, YARA scanning, crypto-constant discovery, driver and heap analysis, and anti-anti-debug helpers. Many projects are Python- or IDAPython-based and integrate frameworks such as Unicorn, Keystone, angr, and ChatGPT-assisted decompilation helpers. Aimed at reverse engineers, malware analysts, exploit developers, and game-security researchers extending disassemblers and debuggers for binary analysis and vulnerability research. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/fr0gger__awesome-ida-x64-olly-plugin.md)

Link catalog rather than a single plugin binary; complements community plugin indexes such as [[idaplugins-list]] and deployment bundles such as [[retoolkit]] for standing up an RE toolchain.
