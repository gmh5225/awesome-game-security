# frida-detection

Java/C++ defensive research repo under Anti Cheat → Detection:Frida focused on detecting [[frida]] dynamic instrumentation. Centers on hooking-based presence checks rather than stealth Frida repackagers such as [[fridare]]. Useful for anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers studying Frida detection tradeoffs on mobile and native targets.
