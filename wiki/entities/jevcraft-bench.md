# JevCraft Bench

Behavioral anti-cheat **research bench** for **Minecraft Paper** servers. Collects **mining telemetry**, runs **typed LLM evaluation**, and supports **offline X-Ray heuristic benchmarking in shadow mode**—useful for measuring detector sensitivity without live enforcement. (source: wiki/sources/README-categories.md)

## Scope

Targets game-security researchers and Paper server operators evaluating behavioral mining-cheat detectors (X-Ray heuristics, telemetry-driven scoring, LLM-assisted review) before deploying production bans.
