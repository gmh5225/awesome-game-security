# logitech-cve (ekknod)

**logitech-cve** is a **Logitech-focused** offensive research project written in **C** and **C/C++**, centered on **driver development** for game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying cheat / **triggerbot & aimbot** input paths. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ekknod__logitech-cve.md)

Sits in the vendor-driver **HID input** lane catalogued under [[hardware-input-injection]]—alongside G HUB/LGS internal move APIs and MouClass kernel samples such as [[kernel-mouse]]—when threat-modeling aim/trigger execution that avoids direct user-mode injection APIs.
