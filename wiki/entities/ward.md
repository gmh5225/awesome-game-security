# ward

Simple ELF runtime packer for creating stealthy droppers. Implemented in Go and C with a focus on modding and memory analysis. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers studying packed ELF clients and dropper staging under Anti Cheat → Binary Packer, rather than shipping as an AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ex0dus-0x__ward.md)

Useful as a Linux/ELF runtime-packing reference alongside [[elfpacker]], [[woody-woodpacker]], and [[m0dern-p4cker]]—not a full unpacker or commercial protector.
