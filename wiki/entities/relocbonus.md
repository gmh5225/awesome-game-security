# RelocBonus

PE relocation–focused research tool (“Attack Reloc”) presented at DEF CON 26. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers studying how relocation metadata can be abused or hardened in the Anti Cheat → Obfuscation Engine lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/nickcano__RelocBonus.md)

Useful as a reloc-attack / PE-structure reference alongside post-compile obfuscators such as [[alcatraz]] / [[binprotect]] and engines such as [[wprotect]]—not a full obfuscation product or unpacker.
