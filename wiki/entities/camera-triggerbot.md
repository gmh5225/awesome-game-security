# Camera Triggerbot

Camera-based triggerbot that reacts to **movement or color** in a region around the crosshair. Listed under cheat / triggerbot & aimbot; useful for researchers studying zero-memory visual offensive pipelines without classic RPM, injection, or trained object-detection models. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/lehmenkuehler__camera-triggerbot.md)

Sits beside ML-heavy capture pipelines such as [[yolov5-pubg]] and [[pine]], and simpler heuristic CV alternatives to full YOLO training; defensive counterparts include [[waldo]] and [[aimbot-detection-prototype]] via [[ai-aimbot-detection]].
