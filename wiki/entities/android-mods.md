# Android Mods

**Curated KernelSU/Magisk modules and LSPosed hooks** for HyperOS and AOSP-style Android builds, focused on Play Integrity attestation, Tricky Store bundles, and root-hide patterns used when testing mobile games and banking apps on modified devices. (source: wiki/sources/README-categories.md)

The collection groups all-in-one Play Integrity + Tricky Store module sets, Strong Keybox Tricky Store variants, and related LSPosed hooks so researchers can reproduce common attestation and concealment stacks without hunting scattered release threads.

Listed in the README under **Cheat → Magisk** beside orchestration modules such as [[jerrymanager]] and catalogs such as [[magisk-hub]] and [[zamr]].
