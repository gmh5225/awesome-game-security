# AntiDBG (HackOvert)

Curated **Windows anti-debugging technique collection** from HackOvert for learning and practical testing. C/C++ with x86 and x64 assembly groups checks by method: **memory structure inspection**, **CPU and timing tricks**, **forced exceptions**, and other debugger-detection approaches. Ships a gauntlet-style test application that runs checks sequentially. Best suited for reverse-engineering education, software protection research, and evaluating anti-analysis defenses in game-security contexts. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/HackOvert__AntiDBG.md)

Complements broader technique catalogs such as [[makin]] and [[al-khaser]], integratable libraries such as [[antidbg]] and [[antidbg-baka]], and bypass/hide tooling studied in RE workflows ([[scyllahide]], [[titanhide]], [[gh-anti-debug-bypass-practice-tool]]).
