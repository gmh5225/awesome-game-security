# petoy

C/C++ and JavaScript project focused on PE (Portable Executable), listed under Anti Cheat → Binary Packer with a `[PE]` tag. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers studying packed PE clients rather than shipping as an AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/r0ngwe1__petoy.md)

Useful as a PE packing reference alongside other PE32/X64 packers—not a full unpacker or debugger.
