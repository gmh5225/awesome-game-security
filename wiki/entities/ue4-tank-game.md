# UE4 Tank Game

Unreal Engine 4 tank game prototype mixing C++ gameplay logic with Blueprint scenes and assets. Implements core classes including a tank pawn, player controller, and AI controller with aiming and crosshair-based targeting flow. Ships multiple maps and packaged art content for iterative gameplay testing. Educational sample for learners studying UE4 architecture, controller patterns, and game prototype development—not a cheat or anti-cheat artifact.
