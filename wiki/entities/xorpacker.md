# xorPacker

Simple C# PE packer that XOR-ciphers executables (works with all PE files). Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers studying packed PE clients under Anti Cheat → Binary Packer, rather than shipping as an AC product. Description centers on debugging. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/nqntmqmqmb__xorPacker.md)

Useful as a lightweight XOR PE-packing reference alongside other Binary Packer samples—not a full unpacker or commercial protector.
