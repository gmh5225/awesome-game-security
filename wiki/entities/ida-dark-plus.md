# ida-dark-plus

IDA Pro dark color theme (“Dark+”) with a modern dark palette across disassembly, hex, structures, and all IDA windows. Replaces IDA’s default colors with a dark background and syntax-highlighted foreground for extended static-analysis sessions—aimed at reverse engineers who prefer dark-mode UIs. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__ida-dark-plus.md)

Visual comfort tooling—not an analysis plugin or decompiler extension.
