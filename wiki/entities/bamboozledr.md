# BamboozlEDR

ETW event-generation tool for testing and research: TUI-driven emission of realistic security events across multiple Windows ETW providers to exercise EDR detection, log analysis, and monitoring pipelines. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers in the anti-cheat / information-system & forensics lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/olafhartong__BamboozlEDR.md)

Complements provider/schema browsers such as [[etw-explorer]], ThreatIntel consumers such as [[tietwagent]], and kernel ETW observability stacks such as [[fibratus]]—generation-side tooling rather than a consumer or bypass.
