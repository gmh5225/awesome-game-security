# Embuche

ELF-focused collection of anti-reversing techniques designed to make reverse engineering harder and more painful. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers working in the anti-cheat / binary packer area under Anti Cheat → Binary Packer / `[ELF]`, rather than shipping as an AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/magnussen7__Embuche.md)

Useful as a Linux/ELF anti-RE reference alongside [[elfpacker]], [[elfuck]], [[woody-woodpacker]], and [[m0dern-p4cker]]—not a full unpacker or debugger.
