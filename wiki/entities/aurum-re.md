# aurum-re

**Aurum RE** — Ricochet anti-cheat research repository in the cheat / explore anticheat system:ricochet lane. Centers on reverse engineering and kernel driver development for researchers studying offensive techniques against Activision **Ricochet**-protected Call of Duty titles. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__AurumRE.md)

Complements sibling Ricochet explore surfaces such as [[ricochet-deobfuscator]] (driver deobfuscation) and [[ricochet-disabler]] (disable/bypass monitoring).
