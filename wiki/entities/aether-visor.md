# AetherVisor

AMD SVM hacked-hypervisor testing project documenting how AetherVisor's features are implemented. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers stressing virtualization-abuse surfaces under `Detection: Hacked Hypervisor Testing AMD` — not a production AC component. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__AetherVisor.md)

Complements sibling AMD SVM stress tooling such as [[baresvm]], Intel VT-x construction stacks such as [[hv]] and [[ophion]], hacked-hypervisor plugins such as [[vt-debuuger]], and detectors such as [[hypervisor-detection]], [[hv-detect]], and [[detect-hypervisor-detect-ring-0]].
