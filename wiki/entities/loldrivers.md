# LOLDrivers

Comprehensive catalog of **Living Off The Land Drivers (LOLDrivers)** — legitimately signed Windows drivers that can be abused for malicious purposes. Maintains YAML-based driver metadata, YARA signature enrichment, [[hvci]] driver tracking, Jinja2-based site generation, and validation tools for the driver database. Primary reference for [[byovd]] researchers, threat hunters, and anti-cheat engineers tracking vulnerable and exploitable signed drivers — upstream to scan clients such as [[loldrivers-client]] and HVCI checks such as [[hvci-loldrivers-check]]. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/magicsword-io__LOLDrivers.md)
