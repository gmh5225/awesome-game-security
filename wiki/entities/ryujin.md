# Ryujin

Research-grade **x86 PE bin-to-bin protector and obfuscator** exploring Bin2Bin transformation techniques on compiled Windows binaries. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers working in the Anti Cheat → Obfuscation Engine lane (`[X86 PE BIN2BIN]`). (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/keowu__Ryujin.md)

Useful as a 32-bit PE bin2bin reference alongside x64 rewriters such as [[binprotect]] and virtualizers such as [[nocturne]], and x32 mutators such as [[milfuscator]].
