# AutoGunfireReborn

**AutoGunfireReborn** is a **C#** sample for **Gunfire Reborn** demonstrating **runtime injection of Unity modules** into **Unity IL2CPP** game processes. Centers on modding, Unity IL2CPP analysis, and in-process module load paths for researchers studying offensive techniques on the cheat / game:gunfire reborn lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__AutoGunfireReborn.md)

Useful for comparing runtime Unity module injection against BepInEx-based mod-menu scaffolds such as [[bepinex-il2cppbase]] and title-specific IL2CPP internal samples such as [[fall-guys-sharp]] and [[goose-goose-duck-hack]] on desktop Unity IL2CPP builds.
