# JerryManager

Shell-script **Magisk**, **KernelSU**, and **APatch** module with a browser-based **Web UI** that orchestrates **Play Integrity** bypass and root-detection evasion on rooted Android devices. Targets researchers and advanced users who need to conceal root, tampering, and instrumentation from apps and games that rely on Google Play Integrity and similar integrity checks. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/WajahatNaeem056__JerryManager.md)

## Integrations

Bundles and coordinates Play Integrity Fix, OhMyKeymint, Tricky Store, TEESimulator, and Zygisk Next to manage keyboxes, attestation, and **per-app targeting**. Key features include security-patch and boot-property spoofing, custom-ROM fingerprint cleanup, **banking mode** that removes Frida and other hooking artifacts, and early-boot property injection to present a locked, non-debuggable device profile. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/WajahatNaeem056__JerryManager.md)

Listed in the README under **Cheat → Magisk** beside curated module catalogs such as [[zamr]] and automated PIF profile pipelines such as [[pif-config-generator]].
