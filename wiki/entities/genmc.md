# genmc

IDAPython script/plugin that displays Hex-Rays decompiler microcode intermediate representation. Visual inspection of microcode generated during decompilation helps debug Hex-Rays microcode plugin development and custom decompiler extensions. Aimed at IDA Pro plugin developers and reverse engineers in the cheat / IDA Plugins lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/patois__genmc.md)

Companion to other Hex-Rays-facing IDA tooling such as [[ida-functioncolor]] and [[openlumina]]—focused on microcode IR visibility rather than Lumina connectivity or pseudocode UI polish.
