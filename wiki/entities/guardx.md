# GuardX

**GuardX** (vunlior/guardx) is a **server-side anti-cheat plugin for Minecraft Paper** servers: Java, Maven-built, evaluating movement, combat, inventory, and packet behavior with configurable built-in checks, thresholds, and punishments. It can use **PacketEvents** when present or fall back to a **built-in Netty packet decoder**; supports **Discord** and **Telegram** notifications, persistent violation history, **per-world or permission** check toggles, **PlaceholderAPI**, and an **API for custom checks**. Oriented at multiplayer operators and staff for detection, alerting, and moderation on supported server versions. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/vunlior__GuardX.md)
