# efi-monitor (ekknod)

**efi-monitor** is an offensive research project focused on **hooking `MmCopyMemory` in a PatchGuard-safe way** from the **EFI / pre-kernel driver** lane. It is written in **C**, **C/C++**, and **C++**, and centers on **driver development**, **graphics**, and **networking** for game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying cheat / EFI driver techniques. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ekknod__efi-monitor.md)
