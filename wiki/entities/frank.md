# frank

JavaScript **League of Legends assistant client** positioned as a **WeGame replacement** (gmh5225; cheat / game:lol). Centers on rendering, animation, and asset pipelines for the LoL client experience rather than traditional memory-read cheat scaffolds. Aimed at game security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques and client-side LoL tooling under [[vanguard]]. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__frank.md)

Sits beside [[league-base]], [[hh-lol-prophet]], [[lol-offset-dumper]], [[lol-unpackman]], and [[lol-patcher]] in the LoL offensive research lane, but as a JavaScript launcher/assistant and UI-asset pipeline study rather than C/C++ cheat bases, dumpers, or unpack tooling.
