# Uncloaking-RAID0-HWID-Serials

Research project demonstrating **uncloaking** or reading **RAID0 disk serial numbers** and hardware IDs that HWID spoofers attempt to hide. Shows how anti-cheat systems can retrieve true hardware identifiers from RAID configurations even when spoofing tools modify the visible serial surface. Aimed at anti-cheat developers building HWID detection systems and researchers studying HWID spoofing limitations. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__Uncloaking-RAID0-HWID-Serials.md)

Sits opposite offensive disk-serial spoof samples such as [[hdd-serial-spoofer]] and broader HWID spoofers such as [[hwid-spoofer]] / [[hwidspoofer]] in the Detection:HWID lane, alongside SMBIOS checkers such as [[hwid-checker-mg]] and TPM spoof detectors such as [[detect-tpm-spoofing]].
