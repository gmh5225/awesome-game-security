# LOST-ARK-SDK

**Generated full-game Unreal Engine-style SDK** for **Lost Ark** (gmh5225). Includes `GObjects`, `GNames`, `ProcessEvent`, `UEngine`, and `UWorld` offsets for `EFEngine.dll`, plus `LA_Basic`, `LA_Core`, and related class, struct, and function headers produced with KN4CK3R sdkgen. Intended for educational reverse-engineering and research in the cheat / game:lostark lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__LOST-ARK-SDK.md)

Complements live-process dump tooling such as [[lost-ark-dumper]] with a pre-built header kit, and sits beside in-process cheat samples such as [[lost-ark]] and wire-protocol tooling such as [[lost-ark-logger]] in the gmh5225 Lost Ark research lane. See [[unreal-object-model]] and generic UE header kits such as [[ue-unreal-engine-sdk]].
