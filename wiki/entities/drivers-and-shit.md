# drivers_and_shit

Large categorized collection of **Windows kernel driver binaries** (`.sys` files) gathered from many hardware vendors and software products—not source code. Serves as a reference corpus of third-party drivers from system utilities, OEM tools, security software, and other applications that commonly expose kernel interfaces. Useful for vulnerable-driver research, exploit reproduction, and anti-cheat bypass or hardening studies alongside curated inventories such as [[loldrivers]], [[ms-vulnerable-driver-list]], and [[physmem-drivers]]. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/alfarom256__drivers_and_shit.md)
