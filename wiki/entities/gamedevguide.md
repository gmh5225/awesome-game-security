# gamedevguide

Personal game-development notes consolidated from years of internal studio onboarding into a browsable Markdown documentation site. Coverage spans Unreal Engine 4 (environment setup, gameplay programming, networking and replication, rendering, shaders, performance optimization, editor tooling), Houdini, graphics theory, and general programming cheatsheets for C++, Python, and related tools. Also includes Windows internals, debugging workflows, and developer-tooling guidance. Intended for MkDocs or Obsidian-style local browsing by game programmers and technical artists—not a cheat, dumper, or anti-cheat artifact. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ikrima__gamedevguide.md)

Useful UE4 and graphics reference beside curated indexes such as [[unreal-engine-guide]], [[ue4-tutorials]], and cross-discipline lists such as [[learn-awesome-gamedev]] and [[anything-about-game]]. Windows internals and debugging sections overlap the [[overviews/reverse-engineering]] lane for analysts studying shipped UE titles.
