# LightweightVK

Deeply refactored bindless-only fork of IGL designed to run on Vulkan 1.3 with optional mesh shaders and ray tracing support. Aimed at low-level graphics programmers and performance-focused engine developers in the Vulkan/API space. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/corporateshark__lightweightvk.md)

Sits in the Vulkan / Guide·API library lane—a performance-oriented Vulkan foundation for engine and graphics research rather than a cheat or anti-cheat artifact.
