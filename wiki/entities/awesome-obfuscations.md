# awesome-obfuscations

Curated **awesome list** of code and binary obfuscation tools and projects. Resources are grouped into three lanes: **binary-code obfuscators** (executable and shellcode transforms), **compile-time obfuscators** (source protection during build), and **compiler-based obfuscators** built on LLVM or GCC toolchains. Coverage spans C/C++ libraries, x86 assembly transformers, Go and Rust tooling, and virtual-machine-based protectors. Aimed at developers and security researchers working on anti-reverse-engineering, anti-tampering, and software protection for native binaries and game-security applications. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/killvxk__awesome-obfuscations.md)

Index hub rather than a single obfuscator binary; complements individual engines such as [[alcatraz]], [[kagura]], [[vmprotect]], and compile-time helpers such as [[obfuscxx]] and [[rust-obfuscator]].
