# Dynsec

Dynamic security analysis framework for studying **runtime protection mechanisms** in games and applications. Provides instrumentation and monitoring to analyze **anti-tamper**, **anti-debug**, and **integrity check** implementations at runtime—not a standalone AC product or cheat. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__Dynsec.md)

Complements DBI frameworks ([[frida]], [[dynamic-binary-instrumentation]]), VEH-based protection RE tools ([[veh]], [[veh-dumper]]), anti-debug catalogs ([[anti-debugging]], [[makin]]), and defensive integrity libraries such as [[memory-guard]].
