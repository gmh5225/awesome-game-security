# Lazysight

**Internal Ironsight multihack** (gmh5225; cheat / game:ironsight `[Internal]`). Combines **ESP**, **aimbot**, and miscellaneous features by hooking **DirectX** rendering for overlay drawing and reading in-process **game SDK** structures—entity lists, weapon managers, and **world-to-screen** transforms—for player and weapon state.
