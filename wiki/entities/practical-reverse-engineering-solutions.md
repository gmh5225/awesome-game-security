# Practical Reverse Engineering Solutions

**Practical Reverse Engineering Solutions** is a community solutions repository for exercises in Bruce Dang et al.'s textbook *Practical Reverse Engineering*. It provides worked answers for x86/x64 disassembly drills, ARM assembly problems, Windows kernel analysis questions, and rootkit analysis challenges, with annotated assembly and explanatory write-ups. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__Practical-Reverse-Engineering-Solutions.md)

Aimed at reverse-engineering students working through the book who want reference solutions while practicing static analysis, kernel internals, and rootkit forensics. Pair with hands-on disassembly in [[x64dbg]], IDA, or [[ghidra]] rather than treating the repo as a substitute for working the exercises.
