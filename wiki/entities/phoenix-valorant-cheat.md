# Phoenix-Valorant-Cheat

**Phoenix-Valorant-Cheat** (YMY1666527646/Phoenix-Valorant-Cheat) is a C++ **external Valorant cheat** project focused on **ESP**, **aimbot**, and **rage-mode** style features. It ships as a Visual Studio solution and uses **OpenGL/GLFW** plus **ImGui** for the on-screen menu and overlay rendering. The codebase includes **offset definitions**, **vector math utilities**, and **external memory-interaction logic** typical of Windows game-hacking tools. Framed for **game security research** and experimentation with external cheat architecture in Riot's shooter environment under [[vanguard]]. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/YMY1666527646__Phoenix-Valorant-Cheat.md)

Sits in the user-mode external Valorant lane beside OpenGL overlay samples such as [[valorant-external-1]] and kernel-assisted stacks such as [[valorant-external-cheat]], and beside other YMY1666527646 tooling such as [[ue4-base]] and [[nigusfn]].
