# gh-remake

**gh-remake** (strimpel1) is a **structured game-hacking and reverse-engineering curriculum** that repackages a large tutorial catalog into a **Hebrew lesson site**, **short English lesson videos**, and a longer **“Game Hacking from Scratch”** episode series built from live captures. **Python** drives catalog generation and video builds; lessons combine **Cheat Engine** memory work, small **C++** demos, and **HyperFrames** rendering with **Azure Speech** narration. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/strimpel1__gh-remake.md)

Tracks span foundations through memory scanning, **IDA** and **Ghidra**-style static analysis, injection and hooking, rendering overlays, **kernel** and **anti-cheat** internals, plus crackme and malware topics organized into many learning paths. **Offline single-player** and open-source practice targets support hands-on labs for students studying game security, anti-cheat mechanics, and RE—not a single-purpose cheat binary.

Complements step-by-step labs such as [[intro-to-gamehacking]], Obsidian curricula such as [[re4f]], and book-aligned samples such as [[game-hacking-code]].
