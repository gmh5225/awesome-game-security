# Ares Framework

**C++ and C# framework** (craids) centered on **modding**, **hooking**, and **debugging**. Listed under Anti Cheat → Binary Packer; aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers working in the anti-cheat / binary-packer space rather than shipping as an AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/craids__AresFramework.md)

Useful as a modding/hook/debug scaffold reference alongside other Binary Packer catalog entries such as [[2pack]] and [[packer-tutorial]]—not a standalone packer or commercial protector.
