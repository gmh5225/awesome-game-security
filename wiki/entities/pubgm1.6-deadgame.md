# pubgm1.6-deadgame

Historical **PUBG Mobile 1.6** decompiled dump archive (gmh5225) from a large APK unpack. Preserves **`libtersafe.so`** (Tencent ACE / mobile anti-cheat native library) and **`libUE4.so`** (Unreal Engine 4 native) symbols and functions from that discontinued client build for offline research reference. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__PUBGM1.6-DeadGame.md)
