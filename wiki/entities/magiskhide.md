# MagiskHide

Portable **ptrace-based MagiskHide module** for **Magisk v24.0+**, restoring root-hide after built-in MagiskHide was removed from upstream Magisk. Uses the **DenyList as hidelist** without requiring **Zygisk** enablement, bypassing root detection via **process trace interception** on **Android 11+**. Sits in the Cheat / Magisk stealth lane opposite detectors such as [[magiskdetector]] and [[detection]], beside research samples like [[hideroot]] and DenyList/Shamiko on [[magisk]].
