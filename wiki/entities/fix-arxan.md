# fix-arxan

Research tooling against **Arxan**, a PE file protector: recovers loader information and a decrypted working image for comfortable static analysis. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers in the Anti Cheat → Dump Fix lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/pr701__fix-arxan.md)

Companion to dump-fix / unpack workflows such as [[unmapper]] (header repair) and protector-specific lanes (Themida/VMP): Arxan-focused decrypt/image recovery rather than generic PE header fix.
