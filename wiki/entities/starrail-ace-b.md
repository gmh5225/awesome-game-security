# StarRail-ACE-B

Reverse-engineering analysis of **ACE (Anti-Cheat Expert)** as deployed in **Honkai: Star Rail** (gmh5225; explore anticheat). Documents Tencent ACE protection mechanisms, detection techniques, and bypass methods specific to the Star Rail PC client — including kernel-driver behavior, integrity checks, and monitoring capabilities for anti-cheat researchers studying miHoYo/HoYoverse titles. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__StarRail-ACE-B.md)

Complements offensive Star Rail samples such as [[star-rail]], [[starrail-s-gc]], and [[starrailcopilot]] with defensive-oriented ACE stack documentation. Sits beside other Tencent ACE RE such as [[honor-of-kings-re-research]] (Android `libtersafe`) and [[anti-cheat-amateur]] (evasion research), and kernel-driver analysis tooling such as [[kace]] and [[readphys]].
