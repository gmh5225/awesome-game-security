# underthehoodofexecutables

In-depth guide to how **C programs** become **executable binaries** — compilation, **linking**, **loading**, and **executable file formats** (PE/ELF fundamentals). Targets anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers who need loader, import/reloc, and on-disk vs in-memory image literacy before studying manual-map injection, module integrity scans, or packer/unpacker workflows. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__underTheHoodOfExecutables.md)

Complements PE triage tools such as [[pe-bear]] and [[totalpe2]], manual-map samples such as [[modexmap]] and [[tinyload]], and step-by-step game-hacking labs such as [[intro-to-gamehacking]].
