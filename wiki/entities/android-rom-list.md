# android_rom_list

Curated documentation/reference list for Android custom ROMs in the cheat / Android ROM lane—not a single standalone runtime codebase. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive mobile techniques where custom ROM choice, unlockability, and root-friendly builds often underpin further instrumentation. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/musabcel__android_rom_list.md)

Sits in the Cheat Magisk / Android ROM–root lane alongside OTA partition tooling such as [[payload-dumper]], boot-image utilities such as [[magiskboot-ndk-on-linux]], and recovery device trees such as [[ofrp-device-xiaomi-mondrian]].
