# w1tn3ss

C++/C **dynamic binary instrumentation, analysis, and patching** framework in the Cheat → DBI lane. Centers on modding, hooking, and memory analysis for game-security and reverse-engineering researchers studying offensive DBI techniques. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/redthing1__w1tn3ss.md)

Pairs with other Cheat DBI tooling such as [[cpp-veh-dbi]] (VEH-based lightweight instrumentation), [[smallworld]] (multi-emulator harness), and [[frida]] / [[frida-scripts]].
