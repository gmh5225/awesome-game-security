# hdd_serial_spoofer

C/C++ **HDD serial spoofer** research sample under the cheat / HWID lane. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive hardware-identifier spoofing, especially disk serial surfaces used in ban/HWID tracking. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/namazso__hdd_serial_spoofer.md)
