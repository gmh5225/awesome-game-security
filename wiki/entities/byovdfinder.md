# BYOVDFinder

Identifies LOLdrivers (known-abused signed drivers) that are **not blocked** by the active [[hvci]] policy. Particularly useful for [[byovd]] attack-path research where vulnerable drivers remain loadable despite Memory Integrity being enabled—aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / vulnerable-driver lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ghostbyt3__BYOVDFinder.md)
