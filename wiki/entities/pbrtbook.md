# pbrtbook

Physically Based Rendering: From Theory To Implementation (kanition). Centers on rendering and modding; aimed at graphics programmers and rendering researchers in the Renderer lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/kanition__pbrtbook.md)

Educational PBR reference implementation—not a game engine, Present hook, or cheat overlay—sitting beside soft-raster and ray-tracing courses as a theory-to-code rendering study surface.
