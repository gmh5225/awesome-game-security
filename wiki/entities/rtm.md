# RTM

Realtime Math (RTM): a library of 3D and 4D arithmetic for video games and other realtime apps that need math as fast as possible (README `Mathematics`). Aimed at engine programmers and gameplay or simulation developers working in the mathematics area rather than as a cheat/AC artifact. Complements higher-level constexpr math/physics frameworks such as [[omath]] as a lower-level realtime vector/matrix building block. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/nfrechette__rtm.md)
