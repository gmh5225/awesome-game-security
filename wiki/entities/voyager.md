# Voyager

**Hyper-V hacking framework** for Windows 10 x64 on **AMD and Intel** hosts, supporting builds from **1507 through 2004**. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive Hyper-V techniques in the cheat / Windows kernel explorer lane—not a production anti-cheat component. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__Voyager.md)

Complements Microsoft Hyper-V introspection such as [[hyper-rev]], Type-2 VT-x learning stacks such as [[hv]], hacked-hypervisor detection such as [[hypervisor-detection]] and [[hv-detect]], and Hyper-V stack LPE research such as [[cve-2025-21333]].
