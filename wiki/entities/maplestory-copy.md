# MapleStoryCopy

**MapleStoryCopy** (ZeromaXHe) is a **Godot .NET** recreation effort for MapleStory-style client gameplay. The client is written in **C#** and ships with extensive scene, sprite, and audio assets for character and map presentation. Its structure emphasizes client-side content organization and learning-oriented implementation rather than production deployment. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ZeromaXHe__MapleStoryCopy.md)

Useful for developers studying 2D MMORPG client architecture and content workflows—not live official-client anti-cheat bypass tooling.

Complements Unity MapleStory reimplementations such as [[maple-unity]], HeavenMS and from-scratch client stacks such as [[maplestory-client]] and [[maplestory-heavenclient]], C# GM emulators such as [[maplenecrocer]], and other Godot title ports such as [[unturned-godot]].
