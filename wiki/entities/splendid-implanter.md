# Splendid Implanter

**BattlEye-compatible injector** that operates entirely from **user mode**, exploiting a flaw in [[battleye]]'s user-mode component that should not have been reachable from Ring 3. secret.club research sample in the cheat / explore anticheat system:be lane—mainly useful for game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive injection against BE-protected titles without kernel drivers. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/haram__splendid_implanter.md)

Complements UM shellcode RE via [[be-shellcode]], manual-map injectors such as [[modexmap]] and [[shtreeba]], and kernel-side BE research such as [[goodeye]].
