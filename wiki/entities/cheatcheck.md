# CheatCheck

**Fabric** server-side **Minecraft** mod combining an anti-cheat **moderation toolkit** with a player-to-player **trading system**, requiring **no client mod** for players. Written in **Java** for Minecraft **26.1.x** on Fabric, it lets operators investigate suspects through a `/cheatcheck` **spectate menu**, **safemode** stealth watching, and staff tools such as **freeze**, **vanish**, **inventory inspection**, and **radar**. The mod merges community `/report` flags with automatic **server-side heuristics** for **reach**, **x-ray-style ore mining**, and **speed** violations, then supports configurable offense presets, **temporary bans**, and **pardons** with persistent world storage. Targets Minecraft server administrators and moderators who need practical, vanilla-client-compatible anti-cheat and enforcement workflows on multiplayer servers. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/EliGamer154__CheatCheck.md)

## Moderation and detection

- **Staff spectate workflow** — `/cheatcheck` menu plus safemode for stealth observation without tipping suspects.
- **Operator tooling** — freeze, vanish, inventory inspection, and radar for live investigations.
- **Community reports** — `/report` flags feed into moderator review alongside automated signals.
- **Server-side heuristics** — reach, x-ray-style ore mining, and speed checks without requiring client mods.
- **Enforcement presets** — configurable offense ladders with temp bans, pardons, and persistent world storage.

Vanilla-client-compatible server-side moderation complements physics-prediction Fabric AC such as [[windfall-anticheatf]] and X-ray investigation plugins such as [[antixrayviewer]].
