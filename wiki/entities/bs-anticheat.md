# BS AntiCheat

Lightweight, false-positive-conscious anti-cheat plugin for **Paper 1.21.10+** Minecraft servers, including full **Folia** support. Written in Java, it detects movement, combat, world-interaction, inventory, vehicle, and packet-level cheats such as speed, fly, reach, killaura, nuker, autoclicker, and x-ray mining. Uses transaction-based lag compensation, configurable violation-level punishments with optional setbacks, SQLite logging, and integrations with PacketEvents, Discord webhooks, PlaceholderAPI, and LuckPerms. Aimed at server administrators who want heuristic, tunable cheat detection that can be calibrated for their environment rather than flagging legitimate players by default. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/BoondockSulfur__BS-AntiCheat.md)

## Detection stack

Movement, combat, world-interaction, inventory, vehicle, and packet-level checks (speed, fly, reach, killaura, nuker, autoclicker, x-ray mining); transaction-based lag compensation; configurable violation-level punishments with optional setbacks; SQLite violation logging; PacketEvents, Discord webhooks, PlaceholderAPI, and LuckPerms integrations; false-positive-conscious heuristic tuning for per-server calibration.
