# CirSAT

Circuit-based Boolean SAT solver that reasons directly over AIG (And-Inverter Graph) / DAG logic networks instead of CNF. Supports AIGER and targets hardware verification and combinational equivalence checking. Sits in the Cheat Mixed boolean-arithmetic / constraint-solver lane beside SMT backends such as [[stp]]. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/nbulsi__cirsat.md)
