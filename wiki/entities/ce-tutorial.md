# ce-tutorial

Cheat Engine (CE) tutorial focused on how CE uses CPU registers — clarifying that not all registers serve the same purposes as commonly illustrated in generic diagrams. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the Cheat guide lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/krampus-nuggets__ce-tutorial.md)

Complements broader CE tooling such as [[cheatengine-mcp-bridge]], [[memmcp]], and [[gddumper]], practical cheatsheets such as [[gamehacking-cheatsheet]], and AC-facing CE detection research such as [[cedetector]].
