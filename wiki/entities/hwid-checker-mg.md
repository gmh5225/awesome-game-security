# hwid-checker-mg

HWID checker that enumerates **system manufacturer**, **model name**, and **serial number** identifier ranges—serial numbers are the primary focus. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers in the Detection:HWID lane who need to map SMBIOS-style hardware fingerprint surfaces. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/medievalghoul__hwid-checker-mg.md)

Complements WMI inventory tooling such as [[windows-hardware-info]], GPU/board fingerprint research such as [[nvidiaapi]], and sits opposite offensive HWID spoofers such as [[hwidspoofer]] / [[spoofer-amidewin]].
