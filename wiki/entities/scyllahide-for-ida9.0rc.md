# ScyllaHide-For-IDA9.0RC

**IDA 9.0 SDK-adapted build** of the ScyllaHide anti-anti-debugging suite (C/C++). Ships hook libraries, injector components, and debugger plugins for IDA and other Windows reversing tools. Masks debugger artifacts and bypasses common anti-debug checks in protected binaries—aimed at reverse engineers and malware analysts who need more stable debugging against hostile or obfuscated targets. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/TKazer__ScyllaHide-For-IDA9.0RC.md)

Complements kernel-mode hide via [[titanhide]] and defensive detection samples such as [[scyllahidedetector2]] and [[makin]]; pairs with IDA-centric RE tooling under [[overviews/reverse-engineering]] anti-analysis.
