# nvidia-overlay-hijack

Research sample that hijacks NVIDIA GeForce Experience's in-game overlay render context to draw custom ESP or menu content without creating new overlay windows—reusing the vendor overlay surface to evade anti-cheat monitoring for newly created overlay HWNDs. Useful for studying third-party NVIDIA overlay hijack techniques beside [[mwclap]], [[steam-overlay-x64]], and [[discord-overlay-hook]], not a maintained product.
