# qortime-ai

**Muse explain/red-team and SAM visualization layer** on top of deterministic console **match-integrity receipts** from the ConWan30 cryptographic console anti-cheat research line (README Anti Cheat). AI-generated advisories and visualizations sit beside fixed referee verdicts—they **cannot upgrade or override** deterministic integrity outcomes, preserving human- or rules-bound enforcement as the authority boundary. (source: wiki/sources/README-categories.md)

Pairs with hardware-rooted controller attestation in [[qortroller]] and analyst-facing CS2 observable-data scoring such as [[cs2-tracker]] when studying how LLM narration should stay subordinate to signed or rule-locked evidence.
