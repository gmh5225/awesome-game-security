# Phoenix AC

Self-leaked multi-component anti-cheat stack for FiveM roleplay servers on Windows: C++ usermode client agent, optional kernel-mode driver, TypeScript WebSocket/MySQL backend, Next.js operator dashboard, Lua in-game resource, and signed-artifact updater. Client modules scan overlays, suspicious hotkeys, cheat-related DNS/hosts, unsigned FiveM modules, manual-mapped drivers/IRPs, debugging/RE tooling, vulnerable drivers, and HVCI/boot misconfiguration; telemetry includes detections, screenshots, minidumps, hardware IDs, and alt-account links. Steam/Discord auth, pin enrollment, live sessions, bans, and Discord webhooks support staff workflows and AC architecture study. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/InsideExploit__phoenix-ac.md)
