# CSHackCreator-2-Demo

**CSHackCreator-2-Demo** (gmh5225) is a **no-code cheat generator** for classic **Counter-Strike 1.6**: users configure custom cheats from input without writing source. Primarily **C/C++**, centered on **OpenGL**, **Vulkan**, and **rendering**. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__CSHackCreator-2-Demo.md)

Useful for game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive cheat-generation workflows in the cheat / game:cs1.6 lane—not a maintained product or feature checklist.
