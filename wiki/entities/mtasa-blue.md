# mtasa-blue

Multi Theft Auto: San Andreas (MTA:SA) — open-source multiplayer modification for Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas with full client and dedicated-server stacks for custom online gameplay. Written primarily in C++ with extensive Lua scripting for resources and mods; Premake-based builds on Windows and Linux. The client embeds Chromium (CEF) for in-game browsers, hooks Direct3D and DirectInput for rendering and input control, and ships built-in anti-cheat modules plus hook/DLL analysis utilities. Useful for multiplayer game development and game-security research around GTA San Andreas modding, client integrity, and anti-cheat techniques — as the client platform that server-side SA-MP/open.mp anti-cheat such as [[open.mp-anticheat]] targets.
