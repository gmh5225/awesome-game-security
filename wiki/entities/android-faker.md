# AndroidFaker

**Xposed/LSPosed module** for rooted Android devices that **spoofs device identifiers** to mask real hardware and account metadata. Targets tracking-relevant fields such as **IMEI**, **Android ID**, **MAC-related values**, **SIM identifiers**, and related properties; supports **randomization** and **manual editing** per field. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Android1500__AndroidFaker.md)

Primary use cases are **privacy-oriented device-identity obfuscation** and **testing how apps respond to spoofed identifiers** — including mobile anti-cheat fingerprint probes and Android reverse-engineering workflows on Xposed hosts. README category: Android.
