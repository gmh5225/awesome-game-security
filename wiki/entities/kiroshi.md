# Kiroshi

IDA Pro plugin that detects common Anti-Cheat artifacts in binaries. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers in the cheat / IDA Plugins lane studying offensive techniques against protected clients (RE and academic use). (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/not1cyyy__Kiroshi.md)

Static IDA-side AC-artifact triage—not a full anti-cheat product or runtime detector.
