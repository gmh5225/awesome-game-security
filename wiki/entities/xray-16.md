# xray-16

Improved open-source reimplementation of the **X-Ray engine** used by the S.T.A.L.K.E.R. series (OpenXRay). Maintains compatibility with original game content while adding **64-bit support**, performance optimizations, bug fixes, and mod-creator features. Written mainly in **C++** and targets Windows, Linux, and macOS. Used by the modding and reverse-engineering community to study and extend production game engine internals—not a cheat or anti-cheat artifact. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/OpenXRay__xray-16.md)

Distinct from GoldSrc/Source study surfaces such as [[rehlds]], [[regs]], and [[source-engine]]; this is the **S.T.A.L.K.E.R. X-Ray** lane for open-engine modding and engine-internals research beside catalogs such as [[open-source-engines]].
