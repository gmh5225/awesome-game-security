# PEzor

C++/C PE packer listed under Anti Cheat → Binary Packer; description centers on pezor with a hooking-oriented implementation surface. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers studying packed PE clients / payloads rather than shipping as an AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/phra__PEzor.md)

Useful as a C/C++ PE packing reference alongside other PE32/X64 packers—not a full unpacker or debugger.
