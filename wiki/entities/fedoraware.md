# Fedoraware

Free and open-source **training software** for Team Fortress 2, forked from SE-Owned. Written primarily in C/C++, with emphasis on rendering, networking, and animation subsystems. Framed for game security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / game:team fortress 2 lane under VAC.
