# xigncode-dump

**XIGNCODE client-module dump** archive (gmh5225) for offline reverse engineering of XIGNCODE-protected game clients. Listed under cheat / explore anticheat system:xigncode with a modding focus; aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques against XIGNCODE rather than production cheat distribution. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__XignCode-Dump.md)

Complements full-pipeline static reconstruction archives such as [[xigncode3-blackdesert]], runtime bypass samples [[xigncode3-bypass]] / [[xigncode3-bypass-alternative]], and kernel-driver exploit PoCs like [[xign-poc-april-2026]]—this repo focuses on captured client-module dumps for signature and module-layout RE rather than unpack pipelines, hooking, or `xhunter64.sys` IOCTL primitives.
