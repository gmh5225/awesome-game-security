# Genshin EasyPeasy Bypass

Bypass for **Genshin Impact**'s **EasyPeasy** / **`mhyprot2.sys`** anti-cheat stack that disables or circumvents the miHoYo kernel driver's integrity checks and detection mechanisms so modified game clients can run. Targets the same signed HoYoverse driver surface as [[mhyprot2]] BYOVD research and [[genshin-debugger-bypass]] anti-debug samples, but focuses on client-modification / AC-evasion rather than debugger attach or IOCTL abuse. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__Genshin-EasyPeasy-Bypass.md)

Framed for game-security researchers studying kernel-driver AC bypass against miHoYo protection — complementary to [[mhynot2]] (driver-load circumvention research) and in-process title cheats such as [[genshin-cheat]] that assume a weakened client AC boundary.
