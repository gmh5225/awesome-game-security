# MSDoggy

**Legacy Taiwan MapleStory (TMS) game hack** (Inndy/MSDoggy). Mixes **C/C++** with **inline assembly hooks** for gameplay manipulation, including **mob behavior changes** and **item-related filtering logic**. Reflects older cheat-development practice: low-level patching and optional protector integration. Best treated as a historical reference for MMORPG cheat research rather than a maintained stack. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Inndy__MSDoggy.md)

Sits beside GMS-era trainer toolkits such as [[msb]] and bot frameworks such as [[msc]], and Taiwan live-client corpora such as [[twms-hacking-data]]. Complements version-specific offset tables such as [[maplestory-cms95-client-address]] and CE workflow samples such as [[maplestory-v179-cheat-engine]].
