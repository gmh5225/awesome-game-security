# MagiskDetection

Archive-style collection of Android proof-of-concept apps for detecting root, Magisk, and related tampering environments. Aggregates many APK samples and documents checks such as Zygisk detection, hook and instrumentation detection, bootloader or ROM checks, and root-app discovery. Provides hashes and references so analysts can compare sample behavior across tools. Primarily for mobile security analysts and anti-cheat researchers studying real-world root-detection techniques. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/apkunpacker__MagiskDetection.md)

Sits in the Anti Cheat `Detection:Magisk` / Android-root lane beside focused detectors such as [[magiskdetector]], [[magisk-killer]], and [[root-app-detector]] (same author), and broader environment probes such as [[android-native-root-detector]] and [[detection]]—opposite root-hide tooling such as [[magiskhide]] and [[riru-momo-hider]].
