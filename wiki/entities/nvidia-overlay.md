# NVIDIA Overlay

Research sample that hijacks the NVIDIA GeForce Experience overlay window—locating its rendering surface and injecting custom DirectX draw calls so cheat overlays render through a trusted system process and evade anti-cheat monitoring for newly created overlay HWNDs. Aimed at game security researchers studying NVIDIA overlay hijacking as an anti-cheat bypass technique; closely related to [[nvidia-overlay-hijack]] and other third-party overlay hijacks ([[mwclap]], [[discord-overlay-hook]], [[steam-overlay-x64]]).
