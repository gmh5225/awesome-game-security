# HWID-EclipsedSpoofer-EAC-BE

**Eclipsed** is a **hardware ID (HWID) spoofer** research repo aimed at **Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC)** and **BattlEye (BE)** protected games. It modifies disk serials, NIC MAC addresses, and other hardware identifiers at the **kernel level** to circumvent HWID-based game bans. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__HWID-EclipsedSpoofer-EAC-BE.md)

Sits in the `Cheat > HWID` lane beside sibling gmh5225 EAC/BE kernel spoof samples such as [[hwid-spoofer-eac-be]], [[hwid-kernel-spoofer]], [[hwid-spoofer]], [[hwid-spoofer-ud-fortnite-warzone-apex-rust-escape-from-tarkov-and-all-eac-be-games-imgui-loader-base]], and [[precision-spoofer-cpp]], general Windows spoofers such as [[hwidspoofer]], and Detection:HWID counterparts such as [[hwid-checker-mg]].
