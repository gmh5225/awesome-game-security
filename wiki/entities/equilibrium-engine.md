# EquilibriumEngine

Data-oriented, multi-threaded **C11 game engine** built around an entity-component-system (ECS) architecture with **hot-reloading** of libraries and shaders for fast iteration on gameplay and rendering. **clang** is the primarily supported compiler. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/clibequilibrium__EquilibriumEngine.md)

Aimed at game developers, engine programmers, and graphics researchers in the Game Engine / source lane—not a commercial title or anti-cheat artifact.
