# compact-vm-detector

Compact **virtual-machine detection** component for embedding in Windows C++ applications. A smaller derivative inspired by [[pafish]], focused on lightweight anti-virtualization checks rather than a full research suite. Prioritizes easy integration in Visual C++ environments with minimal footprint. Primary use case: anti-analysis experimentation and quick environment fingerprinting in security-oriented tooling. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/LukeGoule__compact_vm_detector.md)

Complements full anti-analysis testers such as [[pafish]], header-only VM libraries such as [[vmaware]], sandbox demos such as [[anticuckoo]], and curated technique lists such as [[awesome-anti-virtualization]].
