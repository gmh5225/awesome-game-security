# android-modding

Curated **collection of repositories** for Android game modding — cheat templates, IL2CPP tooling, injectors, and related offensive RE artifacts grouped under one umbrella rather than a single tool. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/jbro129__android-modding.md)

Notable included fork: **Il2CppDumper YuanShen** by kagurazakasanae — a modified [[il2cpp]] dumper targeting Genshin Impact's `UserAssembly.dll` method export (sits beside other YuanShen dump forks such as [[il2cppdumper-yuanshen]]). Primary audience: game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying cheat/guide offensive techniques on Android Unity titles.
