# IPAPatch

Tooling to patch iOS apps without jailbreak (C/C++ and Objective-C). Centers on networking, asset pipelines, and editor tooling in the cheat / iOS jailbreak research lane — specifically the non-jailbreak / sideload path for modified IPAs. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/paradiseduo__IPAPatch.md)

Useful for game-security and reverse-engineering study of unsigned or re-signed IPA modification workflows beside jailbreak stacks such as [[dopamine2-roothide]] and iOS mod-menu samples such as [[imgui-ios-mod-menu]].
