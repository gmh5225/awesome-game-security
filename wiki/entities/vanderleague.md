# VanderLeague

**Hypervisor-assisted** League of Legends offensive research project (gmh5225; cheat / game:lol). C/C++ stack centered on **kernel-level work**, **driver development**, and **rendering** for studying below-OS cheat architectures against Riot [[vanguard]] protections. Aimed at game security researchers and reverse engineers working the LoL hypervisor-assisted offensive lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__VanderLeague.md)

Complements in-guest LoL cheat bases such as [[league-base]], offset/unpack tooling such as [[lol-offset-dumper]] and [[lol-unpackman]], and other gmh5225 hypervisor work such as [[hv-detect]] and [[voyager]].
