# Mir3-Zircon

Open-source **engine and tooling stack for The Legend of Mir 3** (Suprcode). Primarily **C#** with client, server, launcher, editor, and shared library projects covering a full MMORPG workflow—networking layers, content/data models, and **DirectX/SharpDX** rendering pipelines. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Suprcode__mir3-zircon.md)

Targets Mir private-server operators and game-dev communities building custom content, server logic, and modernized deployments—not a cheat scaffold, but a buildable Mir 3 client/server reference for protocol, asset, and render-path study.
