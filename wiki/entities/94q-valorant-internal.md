# 94q-valorant-internal

C++ **internal** cheat for Valorant (94q/Valorant-Internal). Modules cover **aiming** (basic, memory-based, and silent aim variants), **ESP visuals** (boxes, chams, glow), **FOV changes**, and **movement-style exploits** (fly, third-person). A configurable hotkey menu drives feature toggles. Framed for game-hacking experimentation and reverse-engineering practice under [[vanguard]]-protected Valorant.
