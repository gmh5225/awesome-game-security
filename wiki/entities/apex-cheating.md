# apex-cheating

**apex-cheating** (cheatingwitdacode) is a bundled **Apex Legends** cheat-resource collection: a **C# HWID spoofer** that modifies hardware identifiers, **batch scripts** for **offset dumps** across multiple seasons, **EAC bypass** automation, and **SVG asset extraction** tools. It is mainly useful for game-security researchers studying Apex offset tracking, HWID spoofing techniques, and [[easy-anti-cheat]] bypass methodologies on EAC-protected clients.
