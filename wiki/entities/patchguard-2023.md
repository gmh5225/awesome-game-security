# Patchguard-2023

2023-era research on Windows [[patchguard]] (Kernel Patch Protection) documenting internal workings, trigger mechanisms, and potential bypass techniques. Covers timer-based verification, context encryption, the protected-structure list, and recovery routines — aimed at Windows kernel researchers studying KPP's evolving protection mechanisms. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__Patchguard-2023.md)
