# cfclap

Cheat or tool for **CrossFire (CF)**, a popular FPS title. Provides gameplay modification features such as **ESP**, **aimbot**, and other combat advantages through **memory manipulation** of the CrossFire game client. Aimed at game-security researchers studying CrossFire client-side protection and cheat-detection mechanisms. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__cfclap.md)

Complements kernel/driver-oriented CrossFire samples such as [[titancf]] in the cheat / game:crossfire lane.
