# ProMBA

MBA deobfuscator combining **program synthesis** and **term rewriting** to simplify Mixed Boolean-Arithmetic expressions back toward their original forms. Applies algebraic simplification, pattern matching, and synthesis-based recovery for **linear and polynomial MBA** transforms common in VM-based protectors and protected binaries. Targets reverse engineers and deobfuscation researchers working MBA-obfuscated code. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/astean1001__ProMBA.md)

Complements algebraic simplifiers such as [[cobra]] and [[mbased]], synthesis tools such as [[qsynthesis]] and [[drill-and-join]], and MBA sample generators such as [[mutaben]] and [[mba-obfuscator]].
