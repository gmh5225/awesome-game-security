# subhook

Super-simple **C/C++ inline hooking library** for Windows, Linux, and macOS — x86 only (32-bit and 64-bit). Listed for game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive hook/trampoline techniques in the cheat / hook lane. Sits beside Detours-family, PolyHook, and plthook-style libraries as a lightweight cross-platform alternative. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__subhook.md)
