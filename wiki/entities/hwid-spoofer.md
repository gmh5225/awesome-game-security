# hwid-spoofer

**HWID (Hardware Identification) spoofer** research sample written in C/C++, aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / HWID lane. Targets **Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC)** and **BattlEye (BE)** as common commercial AC surfaces for hardware-ban evasion research. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__hwid-spoofer.md)

See also the canonical repo page [[hwid-spoofer-eac-be]] for disk-serial / NIC MAC / SMBIOS kernel-hook detail on the same gmh5225 EAC/BE HWID spoofer surface. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__Hwid-Spoofer-EAC-BE.md)

Sits alongside general Windows HWID spoofers such as [[hwidspoofer]], title-targeted samples such as [[hwid-spoofer-for-fortnite-and-valorant]], game-specific samples such as [[wizard101-spoofer]], and Detection:HWID counterparts such as [[hwid-checker-mg]].
