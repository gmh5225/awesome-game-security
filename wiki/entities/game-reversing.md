# game-reversing

Compiled list of personally recommended learning materials for reverse-engineering video games. Most resources target Windows PC games with x86 assembly emphasized over x64 as an easier on-ramp. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / guide lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/kovidomi__game-reversing.md)

Not a tool binary—a curated learning index that complements hands-on courses such as [[mytechnotalent-reverse-engineering]], practical cheatsheets such as [[gamehacking-cheatsheet]], and broader RE awesome lists such as [[reverse-engineering]].
