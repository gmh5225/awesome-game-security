# reGS

Reverse-engineered reimplementation of **GoldSource engine** (Half-Life 1 engine) components by gmh5225. Recreates core engine functionality through reverse engineering of the original binaries, providing source-level understanding of GoldSource networking, rendering, physics, and game module interfaces. Aimed at Source engine historians and modders studying GoldSource engine internals—not a cheat or anti-cheat artifact. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__reGS.md)

Distinct from CS1.6 server game-DLL reimplementations such as [[regamedll-cs]] (`mp.dll` drop-in), HLDS dedicated-server reimplementations such as [[rehlds]] (dreamstalker; builds 6152/6153; DWARF from Linux `engine_i486.so`) (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/dreamstalker__rehlds.md), decompiled/reconstructed rebuilds such as [[goldsource-rebuild]], and Xash3D path-tracing forks such as [[xash-rt]]; this is a **full GoldSrc engine-component** lane for studying HL1-era client/engine internals beside leaked or maintained Source 1 trees such as [[source-engine]] and Orange Box SDK references such as [[source-sdk-orangebox]].
