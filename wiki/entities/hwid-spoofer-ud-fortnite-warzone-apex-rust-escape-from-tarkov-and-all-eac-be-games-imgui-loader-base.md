# HWID-Spoofer-UD-Fortnite-WarZone-Apex-Rust-Escape-From-Tarkov-and-all-EAC-BE-Games-IMGUI-Loader-Base

**Universal HWID spoofer** research repo with an **ImGui loader** UI, aimed at **Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC)** and **BattlEye (BE)** protected titles. Spoofs disk serials, NIC MAC addresses, SMBIOS identifiers, and GPU serials through **kernel-mode** operations to evade HWID-based bans. Named targets include **Fortnite**, **Warzone**, **Apex Legends**, **Rust**, and **Escape from Tarkov**.
