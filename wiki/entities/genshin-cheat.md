# genshin-cheat

Genshin Impact cheat providing **gameplay modifications** — god mode, infinite stamina, no cooldowns, teleportation, and speed hacks — by modifying the Unity client’s memory or hooking **IL2CPP** methods. Must contend with miHoYo anti-cheat; useful for researchers studying Genshin client-side protection and cheat-detection mechanisms. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__genshin-cheat.md)
