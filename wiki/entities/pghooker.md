# PGHooker

C++ research project focused on **Page Guard** (`PAGE_GUARD`) hooking. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive exception-driven hook techniques in the cheat / hook README lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/nelfo__PGHooker.md)

Pairs with related `PAGE_GUARD` / VEH page-protection samples such as [[voidmaw]] (code-hiding) and trampoline/hook libs such as [[polyhook]], [[polyhook-2-0]], and [[ilhook-rs]]—exception-page hooks rather than inline JMP/IAT styles.
