# regta

Reverse-engineered reimplementation of **Grand Theft Auto III** and **Grand Theft Auto: Vice City**. The project has also been ported to Nintendo Switch, PlayStation Vita, and Nintendo Wii U. Aimed at game developers, reverse engineers, and tooling builders working in the Game Develop / source lane—not a cheat or anti-cheat artifact. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__reGTA.md)

Distinct from GTA: San Andreas binary-compatible reimplementations such as [[gta-reversed-modern]] (RenderWare-era SA decompilation beside the original EXE) and multiplayer mod stacks such as [[mtasa-blue]]; this is a **classic trilogy title reimplementation** lane for studying GTA III / VC engine internals and cross-platform porting.
