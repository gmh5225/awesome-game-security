# Il2cppDumpDroidGUI

**Android GUI adaptation of Il2CppDumper** for on-device [[il2cpp]] dumping (Poko-Apps). Ships primarily as **APK releases** with documentation, changelogs, and configuration guidance rather than a fully exposed source tree. Targets **Android 6 through Android 14** on **armeabi-v7a** and **arm64-v8a**, tracks upstream Il2CppDumper updates, and aims at mobile game reverse engineers who want a simplified dumping workflow on Android without a Termux/CLI setup. README lane `[Il2Cpp Dump GUI]`. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Poko-Apps__Il2cppDumpDroidGUI.md)

Complements CLI/static Android dumpers such as [[il2cppdumper]], cross-platform [[il2cpp-inspector]], and live Frida harvesters such as [[frida-il2cpp-bridge]] on the mobile Unity explorer lane. From the same author as [[memkernel]].
