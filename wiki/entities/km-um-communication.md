# km-um-communication

Learning-focused collection of **Windows kernel-mode and user-mode communication** examples. Multiple C and C++ test projects demonstrate different IPC and synchronization strategies between drivers and user processes—request dispatching, event coordination, and controlled cross-boundary data exchange. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/adspro15__km-um-communication.md)

Aimed at reverse engineering and game-security researchers who need practical KM↔UM interaction patterns before studying stealth cheat drivers or anti-cheat kernel modules. Complements call-path reference material such as [[driver-communication-list]] and shared-memory/offensive samples such as [[kernel-payload-comms]].
