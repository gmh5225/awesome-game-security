# GameDev Resources

Curated index of game development resources across art, code, engines, tooling, design, and monetization (Kavex). Organizes links into structured sections and labels items by availability—paid, limited free, free, and open source. Maintained primarily as Markdown documentation with a focus on discoverability rather than executable code; aimed at developers and technical researchers who need a broad reference library for building or analyzing game technology stacks. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Kavex__GameDev-Resources.md)
