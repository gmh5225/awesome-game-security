# WindowProtect

C/C++ research project focused on **hiding HWNDs** from anti-cheat and defensive window-enumeration paths—driver development, modding, and hooking in the cheat / hide lane. Useful for studying win32k-adjacent window-stealth techniques beside [[window-hijack]] and overlay-evasion samples such as [[nvidia-overlay-hijack]], not a maintained product.
