# MatScan

Multi-threaded **Facepunch Rust** material scanner (C++/C) centered on **Unity [[il2cpp]]** analysis and process **memory analysis**. Enumerates in-game materials via parallel scanning for cheat developers and game security researchers studying offensive techniques on Unity IL2CPP titles. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__MatScan.md)

Complements in-process Rust samples such as [[rust-rustinternal]] and external/read scaffolds such as [[rust-external-1]] when the goal is Unity material/asset discovery rather than ESP or hook-based feature modules.
