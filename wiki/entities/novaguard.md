# NovaGuard

**NovaGuard** (Novastudio953/NovaGuard) is a **server-side anti-cheat plugin** for **Minecraft Paper 1.21** servers. Written in **Kotlin**, it ships **44 individually toggleable checks** covering movement, combat, world exploits, macro-style automation, and x-ray mining—each with its own violation-level thresholds, punishments, and ban commands. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Novastudio953__NovaGuard.md)

README category: Anti Cheat / game:minecraft.

## Staff tooling

Built-in moderation includes a suspects GUI, player freeze, ban waves, violation history, player reports, and Discord webhook alerts. Grace periods, lag shields, Bedrock exemptions, and VPN blocking help limit false positives.

## Positioning

Production-oriented **Paper 1.21** server-side AC for administrators needing granular per-check tuning and integrated staff workflows—beside modular plugins such as [[h-ac]], heuristic Paper plugins such as [[bs-anticheat]], and starter frameworks such as [[nova-anticheat]].
