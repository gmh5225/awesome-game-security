# COD-boiii

**Call of Duty: Black Ops III (BOIII)** client modification and research project (gmh5225; Reverse engineering and analysis). Ships game client patches, SDK structures, and tooling for analyzing and modifying the BOIII game engine at runtime. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__COD-boiii.md)

Useful for game security researchers studying T7-era Call of Duty client internals, runtime engine modification, and modding infrastructure beside [[t7-linker]] (GSC/FastFile linking) and older-title tooling such as [[cod7-tools]].
