# unity-apk-reverse

**unity-apk-reverse** (liuyiyi0219-arch) is a **40-chapter hands-on reverse-engineering course** for **Unity Android APK games**, structured as a progressive curriculum with runnable demos and auto-graded homework. It walks learners through every layer of a Unity mobile title—from APK unpacking and signature bypass through **IL2CPP metadata recovery**, **Lua** and **puerts/QuickJS** script extraction, **DEX** decompilation, and **AssetBundle** resource analysis. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/liuyiyi0219-arch__unity-apk-reverse.md)

The stack centers on **Python and Go** tooling (a course builder and local HTTP loader), with practical use of **Frida**, **frida-gadget**, **unidbg**, **jadx**, and related mobile RE utilities across sample target APKs. Aimed at beginners and practitioners who want structured, authorized practice in mobile game security and Unity APK reverse engineering.
