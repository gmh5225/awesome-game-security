Curated awesome list of **injection** resources — techniques, tooling, and references for getting code into remote or local processes. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the `Cheat / Injection:Windows` lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/itaymigdal__awesome-injection.md)
