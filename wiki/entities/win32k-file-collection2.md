# win32k-file-collection2

Versioned **win32k.sys** binary corpus spanning many Windows 10 and 11 builds for offline comparison. A research artifact for the kernel GUI subsystem—not a ready-made analyzer—aimed at engineers studying win32k changes, syscalls, and input-path internals across OS updates.
