# shellcode-factory

C++ **shellcode factory** tooling for building position-independent payloads. Listed under Anti Cheat → shellcode engine & tricks; aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers studying shellcode construction patterns rather than shipping as an AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/lainswork__shellcode-factory.md)

Complements Windows shellcode build frameworks such as [[scfw]], bad-byte banishment such as [[byvalver]], and in-process asm prototyping such as [[quickasm]].
