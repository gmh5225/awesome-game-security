# quickemu

Tooling to **quickly create and run optimized QEMU VMs** for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Originally aimed at fast Linux-distro testing with portable VM configs (e.g. USB or home directory) and no elevated permissions required to run guests. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/quickemu-project__quickemu.md)

Useful for game-security researchers and reverse engineers who need a lightweight QEMU research host in the `Cheat > QEMU/KVM/PVE/VBOX` lane—adjacent to lab helpers such as [[proxmox]] and fingerprint work such as [[qemu-anti-detection]] / [[proxmox-ve-anti-detection]], but focused on quick guest create/run rather than anti-detection or PVE provisioning.
