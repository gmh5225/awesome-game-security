# intro-to-dx12-2nd-edition-revisited

Modernized reimplementation of Frank Luna's *Introduction to 3D Game Programming with DirectX 12* (2nd ed.) chapter demos—reorganized for easier read/build of the full suite. C++20 modules, HLSL shaders, Visual Studio + vcpkg, DirectX 12 Agility SDK, DirectX Toolkit, and Dear ImGui. Covers the pipeline from DirectX math and initialization through lighting, texturing, compute shaders, tessellation, shadows, SSAO, skinned meshes, terrain, particles, and hybrid ray tracing. Aimed at developers and reverse engineers studying how DX12 titles structure rendering, GPU resources, and shader techniques for graphics internals or security research—not Present-hook or overlay tooling. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/yottaawesome__intro-to-dx12-2nd-edition-revisited.md)
