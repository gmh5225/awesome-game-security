# MSFT DriverBlockList

Community mirror and reference for Microsoft’s **Recommended Driver Block Rules** — the platform vulnerable-driver blocklist that Windows enforces alongside DSE and [[hvci]]. Focused on driver-development and defensive research workflows for anti-cheat engineers and security researchers studying blacklisted / abused signed drivers in the [[byovd]] lane.
