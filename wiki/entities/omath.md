# OMath

C++23 constexpr template math/physics framework for games, mods, and cheat development (README `Mathematics`). Covers linear algebra, world-to-screen projection, projectile prediction, and collision (GJK/EPA), with optional AVX2 and no required third-party runtime deps. Ships premade coordinate/camera helpers for Source, Unity, Unreal, Frostbite, IW Engine, CryEngine, and OpenGL, plus PE/ELF/Mach-O pattern scan and optional DirectX/OpenGL hooking and Lua scripting—aimed at overlays, aim helpers, and RE tooling on Windows, macOS, and Linux. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/orange-cpp__omath.md)
