# docker-win

WSL2-focused project centered on **networking** — aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the `Cheat > QEMU/KVM/PVE/VBOX` research-host lane. Complements official [[wsl]] / [[wsl2-linux-kernel]] stacks by targeting WSL2 network plumbing rather than the core subsystem or guest kernel. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/k3v1n1990s__docker-win.md)

Not a production anti-cheat component. Adjacent to other QEMU/KVM/PVE lab hosts such as [[proxmox]], [[quickemu]], and [[mvisor]].
