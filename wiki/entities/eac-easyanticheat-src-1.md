# EAC-EasyAntiCheat-Src-1

Reconstructed or leaked **Easy Anti-Cheat** source representation (gmh5225) for security research. Contains internal detection routines, driver communication protocols, and client-side integrity validation logic used by [[easy-anti-cheat]]—useful for offline study of EAC architecture without live runtime attachment. Sits in the same Reversed Source lane as [[eazy-anti-cheat-src]] but as a separate archive variant. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__EAC-EasyAntiCheat-Src-1.md)

Complements dump/extract utilities such as [[eac-extractor-utility]] and decompile dumps such as [[easyanticheat-reversing]] by providing readable source-level structure of EAC detection and KM↔UM communication paths.
