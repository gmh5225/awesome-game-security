# ida-copilot

**IDA Pro plugin** that brings GPT-assisted workflows directly into reverse-engineering sessions. Written in Python, it integrates with **IDAPython**, **Hex-Rays**, and **LangChain** to analyze pseudocode, inspect symbols, and apply comment or renaming suggestions. Menu actions and shortcut-driven commands support semi-automated function-level investigation for faster triage and interactive AI support while analyzing binaries. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Antelcat__ida_copilot.md)

Sits in the in-IDA LLM-assistant lane beside rename/explain tools such as [[gepetto]], [[ida-gpt]], and [[binarylens]], and broader copilots like [[aether]] and [[idassist]] that add chatbot or RAG workflows.
