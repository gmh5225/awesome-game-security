# Nemesis

Kernel-space process dumping tooling for Windows. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive dump techniques in the Cheat / Windows kernel explorer lane (free/open samples vs commercial dumpers). Complements kernel cross-process R/W primitives such as [[ntmemory]] and explorer/learning material such as [[document]] / [[systeminformer]].
