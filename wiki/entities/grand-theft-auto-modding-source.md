# Grand-Theft-Auto-Modding-Source

**Grand Theft Auto modding source collection** (gmh5225; Game Develop / source). Aggregates modding source code including **re3** and **reVC** engine reimplementations — reverse-engineered GTA III and Vice City source with platform ports, DirectX/OpenGL rendering, and mod-support infrastructure. Aimed at reverse engineers and modders studying classic trilogy engine internals. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__Grand-Theft-Auto-Modding-Source.md)

Distinct from standalone reverse-engineered reimplementations such as [[regta]] (GTA III/VC with Switch/PS Vita/Wii U ports) and [[game-gta-re3]] (re3/reVC decompiled portable C++; PS Vita via CMake), GTA:SA binary-compatible decompilation such as [[gta-reversed-modern]], and GTA V cheat/mod-menu samples such as [[gta5cheat]] and [[grandtheftautov-cheat]]; this is a **curated modding-source collection** lane bundling re3/reVC-era engine code and Vice City snippets.
