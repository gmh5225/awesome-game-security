# DumpIt-mirror

Mirror of Comae's **DumpIt** Windows live memory acquisition utility. Captures a full physical memory snapshot of a running system in one click, writing **raw** or **Microsoft crash-dump** format suitable for offline analysis with [[volatility]], [[volatility3]], or WinDbg. Targets incident responders and forensic analysts who need a portable, easy live-acquisition tool during investigations—upstream of the RAM-image frameworks in the offline memory-forensics lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/h4sh5__DumpIt-mirror.md)
