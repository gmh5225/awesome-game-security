# hm-pe-packer

Tutorial-oriented C++ x64 PE packer and protector built with Visual Studio. Demonstrates how executable packing workflows wrap and protect Windows binaries at load time; includes source and build assets for studying PE structures, packing stubs, and protection mechanics. Listed under Anti Cheat → Binary Packer (`[PE X64]`); aimed at security learners and reverse-engineering practitioners exploring packer design rather than shipping as an AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/TheAenema__hm-pe-packer.md)

Useful as an educational x64 PE packer/protector reference alongside [[packer-tutorial]], [[x64-exe-packer]], and [[atom-pe-packer]]—not a full unpacker or commercial protector.
