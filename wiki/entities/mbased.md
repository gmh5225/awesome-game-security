# mbased

Practical simplifications of Mixed Boolean-Arithmetic (MBA) obfuscation — tooling and techniques to reduce MBA expressions back toward analyzable form. The project targets code that mixes bitwise and arithmetic operators to hinder static analysis, reverse engineering, and tampering. Useful for game-security researchers and reverse engineers working the Cheat Mixed boolean-arithmetic lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/bliutech__mbased.md)

Simplification-side counterpart to MBA generators such as [[mutaben]] and [[mba-obfuscator]], alongside algebraic tools such as [[cobra]] and synthesis passes such as [[qsynthesis]].
