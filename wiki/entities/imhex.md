# ImHex

Feature-rich **hex editor and binary analysis platform** aimed at reverse engineers and low-level developers. Combines advanced byte editing with pattern-language-driven parsing and visualization, data inspection, diffing, cryptographic hashing, integrated disassembly, and a plugin architecture for custom analysis workflows. The codebase is primarily C++ with cross-platform desktop support. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/WerWolv__ImHex.md)

Widely used for firmware, file-format, memory-dump, and game-binary investigation in security research—static triage of packed clients, asset blobs, and cheat samples before deeper IDA/Ghidra work. Complements Qt workbenches such as [[hexwalk]], PE viewers such as [[pe-bear]] and [[totalpe2]], and bundled lab installs such as [[retoolkit]] that ship ImHex alongside other RE tools.
