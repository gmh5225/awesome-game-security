# D3D9On12

Microsoft **Direct3D9-On-12** mapping layer: translates legacy Direct3D 9 workloads onto Direct3D 12 rather than exposing a standalone D3D9 API. The project implements the **D3D9 usermode DDI** (device driver interface)—the boundary between the D3D9 runtime and the driver—so D3D9-era titles and tooling can run on D3D12-only paths. Aimed at graphics programmers and Windows game-tooling developers in the DirectX / compatibility lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/microsoft__D3D9On12.md)
