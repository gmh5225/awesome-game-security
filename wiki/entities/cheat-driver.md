# cheat-driver

Kernel research sample focused on **`MmCopyVirtualMemory` testing**: a minimal own kernel-mode driver path for cross-process memory access, framed as one of the simplest Ring0 approaches past usermode anti-cheat handle/RPM constraints. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers in the Anti Cheat → stress-testing lane (adjacent to [[ntmemory]], [[evcommunication]], [[read-write-driver]]).
