# WineHooks

C/C++ **game compatibility and enhancement framework** that injects hooks into **legacy PC titles**. Combines **DirectX and OpenGL interception**, resolution scaling, texture replacement, audio and input fixes, and many **game-specific patch profiles** for modern systems. Mixes native components with automation and configuration assets; runs on **native Windows** and **Linux through Wine**. Used mainly by modders and reverse-engineering practitioners who need practical hooks for restoring or extending old game behavior. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Daniel-Lobo__WineHooks.md)
