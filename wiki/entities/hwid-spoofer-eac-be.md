# Hwid-Spoofer-EAC-BE

**Hwid-Spoofer-EAC-BE** is a C/C++ **hardware ID (HWID) spoofer** research repo aimed at **Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC)** and **BattlEye (BE)**. It spoofs disk serials, NIC MAC addresses, and SMBIOS identifiers through **kernel-mode driver hooks** to evade HWID-based bans in protected games. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__Hwid-Spoofer-EAC-BE.md)

Sits in the `Cheat > HWID` lane beside sibling gmh5225 samples such as [[hwid-spoofer]], [[hwid-kernel-spoofer]], [[hwid-spoofer-ud-fortnite-warzone-apex-rust-escape-from-tarkov-and-all-eac-be-games-imgui-loader-base]], [[precision-spoofer-cpp]], and [[uncloaking-raid0-hwid-serials]], general Windows spoofers such as [[hwidspoofer]], title-targeted kernel spoof research such as [[hwid-spoofer-for-fortnite-and-valorant]], and Detection:HWID counterparts such as [[hwid-checker-mg]].
