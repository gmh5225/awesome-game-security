# EC_PRO-LAN (ekknod)

**EC_PRO-LAN** is an offensive game-security research project written in **C++** and **C/C++**, centered on **anti-cheat research**, **driver development**, and **OpenGL**, for researchers studying the cheat / explore anticheat **system:faceit** area. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ekknod__EC_PRO-LAN.md)

Documented lab requirements emphasize a constrained host profile: **Windows 10 Enterprise 1607 LTSB** (fully updated), **AMD Ryzen** CPU, **B350–B450** chipset motherboards (notes **B550 client is bugged**), and **Logitech G HUB** installed for mouse input—reflecting hardware/platform coupling common in kernel + HID cheat stacks studied beside [[logitech-cve]] and [[kernel-mouse]].
