# Anti-Cheat-TestBench (ekknod)

**Anti-cheat testBench harness** (ekknod) written in **C++** and **C/C++**, centered on **anti-cheat research**, **driver development**, and **hooking** for the Open Source Anti Cheat System lane. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers building or evaluating OSS AC detection coverage. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ekknod__Anti-Cheat-TestBench.md)

Complements modular coverage-testing frameworks such as [[anti-cheat-testing-framework]], educational detector PoCs such as [[anticheat-poc]], and sibling ekknod OSS AC samples such as [[csgo-ac]] for systematic AC evaluation beside kernel/driver research like [[efi-monitor]] and [[nmi]].
