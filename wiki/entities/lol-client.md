# lol-client

Custom or modified **League of Legends client** implementation for research (gmh5225; cheat / game:lol). Provides alternative client functionality or protocol implementation for analyzing LoL **client-server communication** and **game state management** under [[vanguard]]-protected Riot clients. Aimed at game security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques and LoL wire/state semantics rather than in-guest memory-read cheat scaffolds. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__LoLClient.md)

Complements [[frank]], [[lol-patcher]], [[lol-unpackman]], and [[lol-offset-dumper]] in the LoL offensive research lane, but as a full client/protocol study substrate rather than patcher, unpack, dump, or assistant-launcher tooling.
