# tx-holdem

**tx-holdem** — JavaScript library for building Texas Hold'em poker logic in Node.js and browsers. Models cards and hands, compares combinations, selects the highest hand, and exposes draw-related calculations through a reusable API. Ships with examples and tests demonstrating programmatic board/pocket-card composition and ranking workflows. Aimed at game developers and simulation tool authors who need a lightweight engine for poker gameplay rules. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Merisho__tx-holdem.md)
