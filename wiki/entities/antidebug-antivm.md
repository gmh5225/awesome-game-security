# AntiDebug-AntiVM

Reference **anti-debugging** and **VirtualBox anti-VM** technique examples focused on the **Vbox** lane. Not intended as a standalone build — the repo shows how to integrate individual checks into existing projects. Aimed at **anti-cheat engineers** and **defensive security researchers** studying debugger resistance and `Detection:Virtual Environments` probes. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__AntiDebug-AntiVM.md)

Complements broader anti-debug catalogs such as [[makin]] and [[anti-debugging]], VM-detection libraries such as [[vmaware]], and BH2012 PoCs [[blackhat2012]]; pairs with guest fingerprint spoof research such as [[qemu-patched]] and [[vmware-hardened-loader]] on the evasion side.
