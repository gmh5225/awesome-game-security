# BYOUD

**Framework for x64 stack spoofing on Windows** — takes the opposite approach from classic return-address / synthetic-frame spoofing by **manipulating unwind metadata** to hide arbitrary chunks of the call chain from debuggers and EDRs. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive cheat / spoof-stack tradecraft in the `Cheat > Spoof Stack` lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/klezVirus__BYOUD.md)
