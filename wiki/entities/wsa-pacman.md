# WSA PacMan

Flutter/Dart **GUI package manager and installer** for Android apps on **Windows Subsystem for Android (WSA)**. Supports double-click installation of **APK** and **XAPK** packages, displays metadata such as version and permissions, and handles app upgrades or downgrades. Quick controls open Android settings and app-management pages inside WSA. Aimed at users and testers who sideload and manage Android apps on Windows efficiently. Sits in the README `WSA` lane beside MSIX patchers such as [[wsapatch]], CLI installers such as [[win11-apk-installer]], and rooted builds such as [[magiskonwsalocal]].
