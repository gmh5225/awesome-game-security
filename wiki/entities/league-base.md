# league-base

C++ **League of Legends cheat base framework** (gmh5225; cheat / game:lol). Provides memory-reading infrastructure, game-object enumeration, champion data extraction, and rendering-overlay foundations for the LoL client, with Packman/Vanguard anti-cheat considerations and LoL custom engine structures. Aimed at game security researchers studying LoL cheat architecture and Riot anti-cheat protections. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__league-base.md)

Sits beside [[lol-offset-dumper]], [[lol-unpackman]], and [[lol-patcher]] in the LoL offensive research lane under [[vanguard]], but as a full cheat **base scaffold** rather than dump-only or unpack tooling.
