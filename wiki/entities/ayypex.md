# ayypex

Linux **internal** Apex Legends cheat research repo (gmh5225). Renders in-game UI via a **Vulkan implicit layer** directly on the game's window—same injection pattern as MangoHud overlays rather than a separate transparent HWND or D3D Present hook. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / game:apex legends lane on Linux (Proton/Wine clients). (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__ayypex.md)
