# OpenClaw

Open-source C++ reimplementation of the classic Captain Claw (1997) platformer with Box2D physics. Cross-platform via CMake (including Android build configs) and recreates original levels, actors, and gameplay. Aimed at game-engine researchers and modders studying 2D platformer remakes and custom engine architecture with physics—not a cheat or anti-cheat artifact. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/pjasicek__OpenClaw.md)

Useful as a readable classic-title remake surface in the Game Develop / source lane, adjacent to other remakes ([[zelda3]], [[osu]]) and approachable C/C++ gamedev samples ([[raylib]], [[2048-cpp]]).
