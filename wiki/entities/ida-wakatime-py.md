# ida-wakatime-py

IDA Pro **WakaTime integration plugin** (Python; es3n1n) that runs in the background and sends heartbeat events to the WakaTime API, automatically reporting time spent on reverse-engineering tasks and tracking which binaries are being analyzed. Aimed at reverse engineers who use WakaTime to track productivity across development and analysis tools. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/es3n1n__ida-wakatime-py.md)

Not an analysis or deobfuscation tool—scoped as workflow/time-tracking for IDA sessions.
