# DELBOT-Mouse

Deep learning project to **distinguish human vs bot mouse movements**. Initiated during a Bureau404 internship as part of the University of Mons Master's in Mathematics (Specialist Focus on Careers in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence). Useful for anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers in the Detection:triggerbot & aimbot lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/chrisgdt__DELBOT-Mouse.md)

Sits opposite offensive human-like movement samples such as [[human-mouse-movement]] and beside behavioral ML detectors catalogued under [[ai-aimbot-detection]] and [[aimbot-detection-prototype]].
