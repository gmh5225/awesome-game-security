# anti-cheat

C/C++ **open-source anti-cheat system** listed under Anti Cheat → Open Source Anti Cheat System, centered on anti-cheat research. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers studying client protection stacks rather than at cheat development. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/jnastarot__anti-cheat.md)

Part of the jnastarot open-source AC toolchain alongside [[ice9]] (OSS AC research), [[shibari]] (PE merge/link), and [[furikuri]] (obfuscation engine)—not a commercial AC product.
