# CPUEngine

Real-time 3D software renderer that runs entirely on the CPU. Written in C++17 with SDL2; implements rasterization, depth testing, multiple shading models, post-processing effects, and OBJ/TGA asset loading. Offers scalar and SIMD rendering paths, thread-pool-based triangle work distribution, and interactive camera and HUD controls. Primarily intended for graphics education and low-level rendering performance research rather than as a cheat or anti-cheat artifact. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/FHowington__CPUEngine.md)
