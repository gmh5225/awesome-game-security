# PEPacker

C++ PE X64 binary packer listed under Anti Cheat → Binary Packer (`[PE X64]`). Centers on asset-pipeline workflows for packing x64 PE clients—aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers studying binary packers rather than shipping as an AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/hid3rx__PEPacker.md)

Useful as an x64 PE packer reference alongside [[x64-exe-packer]] and PE triage tooling—not a full unpacker or debugger.
