# Tales of Cheat Tables

**Tales of Cheat Tables** (Sora3100/Tales_of_Cheat_Tables) is a curated library of **Cheat Engine** tables for many Bandai Namco **Tales** series titles on **PC**, native console builds, and **emulator** targets. Each table is XML **cheat table** (`.CT`) content that combines **Auto Assembler** with embedded **Lua** to resolve process-specific module bases, **AOB** scans, and fixed offsets so modders and researchers can inspect and edit party data, stats, skills, items, and other in-memory JRPG state. The repository also ships companion **emulator patches and configs** for **PCSX2**, **RPCS3**, **PPSSPP**, and **PlayStation Vita** tooling (YAML RPCS3 patches, pnach/ini files, Vita multiplayer and vitacheat assets), with per-game feature notes and troubleshooting. The collection targets offline modding, trainer prototyping, and study of how classic Tales binaries expose mutable save and runtime layouts under emulation. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Sora3100__Tales_of_Cheat_Tables.md)
