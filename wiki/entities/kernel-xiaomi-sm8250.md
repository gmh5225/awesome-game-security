# kernel_xiaomi_sm8250

Custom Android kernel source for **Xiaomi SM8250** (Qualcomm Snapdragon 870/888) devices, including **POCO F3** and **POCO F4**. Includes AnyKernel3 flashable packaging, device-specific defconfigs, GitLab CI integration, and GKI ABI definitions for Qualcomm platforms—aimed at Android kernel developers and mobile security researchers building or analyzing custom kernels on SM8250 hardware. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Danda420__kernel_xiaomi_sm8250.md)

Stock GKI OEM trees like this sit beside peer SM8250 sources such as [[android-kernel-oneplus-sm8250]], other Qualcomm Xiaomi kernels such as [[android-kernel-xiaomi-sm8475]] and [[android-kernel-xiaomi-sweet]], and branch-index catalogs such as [[xiaomi-kernel-opensource]] and [[kernel-devicetree]].
