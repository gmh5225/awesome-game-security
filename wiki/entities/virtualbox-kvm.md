# virtualbox-kvm

**VirtualBox with a KVM backend** — a VirtualBox fork that runs guests on Linux **KVM** instead of the stock VirtualBox hypervisor. **Hardware graphics acceleration** is not wired into the GUI yet and needs **manual setup**. Primarily useful for **game-security researchers** and **reverse engineers** studying offensive techniques in the `Cheat > QEMU/KVM/PVE/VBOX` research-host lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/cyberus-technology__virtualbox-kvm.md)
