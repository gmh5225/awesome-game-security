# Pcileech Activator Anti-crack (Herooyyy)

**Research corpus on [[pcileech]]-class FPGA DMA firmware activator protection and cracking** for commercial panel-licensed bitstreams. Ships **SystemVerilog** for **FT601** basic and advanced [[pcileech-fpga]] configurations, **C++ activation-signal analysis** code, and documentation on **signal registers**, **state machines**, and **detection flags** used by commercial firmware activator panels. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Herooyyy__Pcileech-Activator-Anti-crack.md)

## Research focus

Targets DMA security researchers studying how **vendor firmware licensing** gates bitstream use on FPGA DMA hardware—not PCIe class-emulation or anti-cheat evasion profiles. Complements behavioral firmware forks such as [[pcileech-intel-i226-v-fullemu]] and [[pcileech-isabridge]] from the same author lane by exposing the **activation handshake layer** beneath those releases.
