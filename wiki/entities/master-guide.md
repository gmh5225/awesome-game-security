# master-guide

Structured **CS:GO guide** from csgohacks, organized into **five main parts**, aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / guide lane—not a standalone runtime cheat binary. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/csgohacks__master-guide.md)

Complements hands-on CS:GO scaffolds such as [[csgo-cheat-base]], [[csgo-internal-base]], and [[osiris]], SDK/header work such as [[csgo-sdk]] and [[source-netvars]], and broader game-hacking coursework such as [[intro-to-gamehacking]] and [[gamehacking-cheatsheet]].
