# godot-plush-character

Godot 4.x plugin or demo for **plush / soft-body character rendering** with a stylized stuffed-animal look. Demonstrates shader-based fabric texturing, soft deformation, and procedural animation for appealing plush-style 3D characters—aimed at Godot developers and technical artists building stylized character rendering effects rather than shipped-title RE. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gtibo__Godot-Plush-Character.md)

Sits in the Godot **Plugins / stylized rendering** lane beside authoring MCP such as [[better-godot-mcp]] and sandboxed mod addons such as [[godot-sandbox]]; complements educational shader courses such as [[3d-game-shaders-for-beginners]] on the fabric/deformation shader side.
