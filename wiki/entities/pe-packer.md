# PE-Packer

C/C++ x86 PE binary packer listed under Anti Cheat → Binary Packer (`[X86]`). Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers studying binary packers and packed-client analysis rather than shipping as an AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/czs108__PE-Packer.md)

Useful as an x86 PE packer reference alongside [[packer]] and [[pe32-password]]—not a full unpacker or debugger.
