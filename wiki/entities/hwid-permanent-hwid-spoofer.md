# HWID-Permanent-HWID-Spoofer

**HWID-Permanent-HWID-Spoofer** is a **permanent hardware ID (HWID) spoofer** research repo that persists spoofed identifiers across system reboots. It modifies firmware-level hardware identifiers through **kernel driver** or **registry manipulation**, maintaining spoofed values without requiring re-execution after each boot. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__HWID-Permanent-HWID-Spoofer.md)

Sits in the `Cheat > HWID` lane beside session-oriented gmh5225 spoofers such as [[hwid-spoofer]], [[hwid-spoofer-eac-be]], and [[precision-spoofer-cpp]], universal loader bases such as [[hwid-spoofer-ud-fortnite-warzone-apex-rust-escape-from-tarkov-and-all-eac-be-games-imgui-loader-base]], and Detection:HWID counterparts such as [[hwid-checker-mg]].
