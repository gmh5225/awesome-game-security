# m0dern_p4cker

C/C++ ELF-focused binary packer (README tag `[ELF]`). Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive researchers studying packed ELF clients under Anti Cheat → Binary Packer, rather than shipping as an AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/n4sm__m0dern_p4cker.md)

Useful as a Linux/ELF packer reference alongside [[woody-woodpacker]] and [[elfuck]]—not a full unpacker or debugger.
