# orress

**ORRESS** (Okiedocus Rusty Reverse Engineering Software Suite) is a pure-Rust integrated RE environment for PE, ELF, Mach-O, .NET, Java, DEX, WebAssembly, and related formats. It combines disassembly, CFG/xrefs, a decompiler that can emit compilable Rust, native debugging, an x86-64 emulator with time-travel tracing, symbolic solving, YARA-X malware triage, and patch/assembler workbenches. Zed-style extensions and Rhai scripting support automation; **ORRESS Academy** bundles guided lessons and crackmes. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/okiedocus__orress.md)
