# Spoofer

**Spoofer** (AlfredIU) is a Windows **C++ game-hacking package** that combines a **user-mode overlay cheat** with **driver-side components**. The user-mode code includes modules such as **aimbot**, **ESP visuals**, **entity caching**, and **projectile prediction**, rendered through a **DirectX 9 ImGui** interface. The repository also contains **kernel driver build artifacts** and spoofing-oriented branding, indicating a mixed focus on live cheating and **identity evasion** workflows. It is mainly relevant for studying offensive game-cheat architecture and anti-cheat bypass tradecraft. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/AlfredIU__Spoofer.md)

Sits in the `Cheat > HWID` lane beside ImGui-loader HWID spoofer bases such as [[hwid-spoofer-eac]] and [[imgui-spoofer-leaked]], and beside bundled overlay cheat + HWID samples such as [[fortnite-external-5]] and [[valorant-cheat-external]].
