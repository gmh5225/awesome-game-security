# PrecisionSpoofer-CPP

**PrecisionSpoofer-CPP** is a C++ **HWID (hardware identification) spoofer** research repo centered on **kernel driver development** for Windows. Listed under cheat / HWID; aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive hardware-ban evasion tradecraft. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__PrecisionSpoofer-CPP.md)
