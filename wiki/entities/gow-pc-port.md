# God of War PC Port (gow-pc-port)

**gow-pc-port** ([danielalves96/gow-pc-port](https://github.com/danielalves96/gow-pc-port)) is a **static recompilation** effort targeting **God of War (PS2, SCUS-97399)**, lifting retail MIPS into **native C++** with an **OpenGL** host for **Windows and Linux**. A **Rust MIPS recompiler** drives the lift; the repo documents the **RE and porting pipeline** and ships **no bundled game assets** (bring-your-own disc). (source: wiki/sources/README-categories.md)

## Research use

Fits the README **Cheat → Guide / RE Tools** lane beside other console lift-and-port projects such as [[ps2-port-engine]] and [[static-runtime-evidence]]-style verification workflows—preservation and interoperability study, not live-service cheat delivery.
