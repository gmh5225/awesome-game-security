# KiSystemStartupMeme (ekknod)

**KiSystemStartupMeme** is an offensive research project focused on a **custom `KiSystemStartup`** hook — early kernel-init path manipulation from the **EFI / pre-kernel driver** lane rather than a conventional signed `.sys` load. It is written in **C**, **C/C++**, and centers on **kernel-level work**, **driver development**, and **graphics** for game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying cheat / EFI driver techniques. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ekknod__KiSystemStartupMeme.md)
