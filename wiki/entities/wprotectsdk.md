# WProtectSDK

C/C++ **WProtect SDK** project centered on SDK generation for obfuscation-engine integration. Listed under Anti Cheat → Obfuscation Engine; aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers studying protector SDK surfaces rather than shipping as a commercial AC product. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/jokerNi__WProtectSDK.md)

Distinct from the separate [[wprotect]] obfuscation-engine reference (`xiaoweime/WProtect`); useful alongside LLVM pass plugins and bin2bin virtualizers when studying how protection SDKs expose markers, hooks, or build-time integration points.
