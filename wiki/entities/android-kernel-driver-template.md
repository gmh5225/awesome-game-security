# android-kernel-driver-template

**GKI Android kernel driver (AArch64) template** for building out-of-tree modules against a **product kernel** (also called a device or OEM kernel—the kernel shipped on hardware). Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in cheat / Android kernel driver development. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__android-kernel-driver-template.md)

Starter scaffold for GKI `vendor_dlkm` / LKM workflows; complements [[compile-android-driver]] (kade build/ABI automation) and research LKMs such as [[android-kernel-hacking-toolkit]] and [[android-touch]] (input-path driver samples).
