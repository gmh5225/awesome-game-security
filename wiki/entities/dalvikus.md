# Dalvikus

Android reverse-engineering tool and smali editor for inspecting and editing Dalvik bytecode at the smali layer. Positioned for ethical research by game-security analysts and reverse engineers studying offensive cheat and RE tooling workflows on Android. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/loerting__dalvikus.md)

Complements DEX→Java decompilers such as [[jadx]], DEX↔JAR bridges such as [[dex2jar]] (`d2j-baksmali`), and agent-facing MCP wraps ([[delamain]], [[apktool-mcp-server]]) when direct smali edit or low-level Dalvik inspection is needed beside higher-level Java recovery.
