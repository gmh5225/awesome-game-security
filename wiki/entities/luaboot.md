# luaboot

Fully **scriptable UEFI bootloader** (MIT). Targets game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / EFI driver area — pre-OS boot logic can be driven from Lua scripts rather than fixed C payloads, complementing mapper scaffolds such as [[uefi-bootloader]] and [[eficmake]]. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/leap0x7b__luaboot.md)
