# KitsuPE

**PE (Portable Executable) parser and editor** for Windows binary analysis. Parses PE headers, sections, imports, exports, relocations, and resources in detail, with **editing support for binary patching**. Listed under the cheat / RE tools lane with a `[PE]` tag. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__KitsuPE.md)

Useful for triage and light patching of game clients, cheat modules, and injected DLLs before deeper IDA/Ghidra work—complements GUI viewers such as [[pe-bear]] and [[totalpe2]], and MSVC Rich Header triage via [[compiler-binary-richprint]].
