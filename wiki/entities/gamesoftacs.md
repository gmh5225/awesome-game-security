# GamesoftACS

Server-side anti-cheat system for Knight Online **Gamesoft** private-server stacks. Implements cheat detection on the game server: speed-hack and teleport checks, abnormal damage validation, inventory manipulation checks, and client integrity verification. Monitors gameplay patterns and flags suspicious activity for operator review. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/luisfelipe18__GamesoftACS.md)

Aimed at game server developers building custom, server-authoritative anti-cheat for multiplayer MMOs—same lightweight server-operator lane as [[maplestory143]] CheatTracker and [[oomph]] proxy AC, rather than kernel products such as [[easy-anti-cheat]] or [[vanguard]].
