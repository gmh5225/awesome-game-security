# RE4F

**Reverse Engineering 4 Fun & Profit** (Coldzer0) is an **Obsidian-friendly educational documentation repository** that teaches **Windows reverse engineering** from fundamentals through practical analysis workflows. Written primarily in Markdown with **C and assembly** examples, it covers **x86/x64** architecture, **NASM and MASM** syntax, **Windows memory management and internals**, and the **Portable Executable** format—including headers, sections, imports, and exports. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Coldzer0__RE4F.md)

The curriculum walks through **static and dynamic analysis** using tools such as IDA, [[ghidra]], [[x64dbg]], WinDbg, Binary Ninja, and DnSpy, and explains **anti-analysis** topics including packing, anti-debugging, and bypass techniques. Intended for learners and practitioners in reverse engineering, malware analysis, and game security who want a structured reference on tools and techniques rather than a single-purpose tool or script.

Complements hands-on course tracks such as [[mytechnotalent-reverse-engineering]], [[hacking-windows]], [[intro-to-gamehacking]], and curated indexes such as [[reverse-engineering]] and [[game-hacking]] (dsasmblr). Same author's tooling includes [[ida-for-delphi]] and [[luadecompiler]].
