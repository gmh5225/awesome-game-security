# Recast Navigation

Industry-standard C++ navigation-mesh toolset (**Recast & Detour**) for games. Recast builds navmeshes from arbitrary 3D geometry via voxelization, contour tracing, and polygon-mesh simplification; Detour runs pathfinding, path smoothing, and local steering on those meshes (off-mesh connections, area costs, tile-based streaming). Used across commercial engines and titles by developers implementing AI navigation. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/recastnavigation__recastnavigation.md)

Sits under README `Game Tools` beside UE builders/profilers such as [[unreal-binary-builder]] and [[unreal-network-profiler]]—engine/AI infrastructure rather than a cheat or anti-cheat artifact.
