# NightSkyEngine

**Free open-source UE5 fighting-game framework** (WistfulHopes) for 2D and 2.5D competitive titles. Implementation is primarily **C++** with engine modules, plugins, and game-side systems tailored for fighters; integrates **GGPO-based rollback networking** and ships tooling such as a **collision editor** for gameplay iteration. Targets developers and technical designers prototyping or shipping modern fighting games on higher-end PC and console hardware—not a cheat or anti-cheat artifact. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/WistfulHopes__NightSkyEngine.md)

Complements other Unreal Game Develop fighting surfaces in the wiki ([[fightinggame-ue5]], [[unrealengine5-ultimate-streetfighters]]) by centering on a full framework with rollback netcode and collision tooling rather than animation BP samples or Street Fighter–style audio/physics integration alone.
