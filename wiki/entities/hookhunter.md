# HookHunter

Multi-purpose Windows tool that scans a target process for patches and hooks, builds a report, and can follow generic hooks to their final destination so analysts see where hooks land without manual tracing. Aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers in the `Detection:hook` lane — complementary to hook-integrity research such as [[hook-buster]], live-vs-disk comparison such as [[patch-finder]], and runtime [[pe-sieve]] scanners such as [[xmalhunter]]. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/mike1k__HookHunter.md)
