# Simple EFT Superman Training Bot

**Arduino-based automation bot** for **Escape from Tarkov** training routines (ZhaoKunqi; cheat / game:eft `[Automation]`). Uses **HID-capable boards** to emulate **keyboard and mouse input** so repetitive in-game movement cycles can run automatically—aimed at hardware-assisted game automation experiments and skill-grinding scripts rather than memory reads or overlays. The repository ships multiple **`.ino` sketches** plus a **Python coordinate conversion helper** for adapting button positions and flow updates. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ZhaoKunqi__simple-eft-superman-training-bot.md)

Sits in the zero-memory automation lane via [[hardware-input-injection]] beside Arduino HID colorbot samples such as [[overwatch2-colorbot-cheats]] and screen-driven copilots such as [[starrailcopilot]]. Complements EFT memory-read and radar corpora such as [[simple-eft-base]], [[eft-external]], and [[escapefromtarkov-trainer]] when studying BattlEye-facing skill-grinding bots vs internal/DMA cheats.
