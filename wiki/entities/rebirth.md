# Rebirth

**MapleStory GMS-095** private-server emulator in **C#** (gmh5225): server-side game logic spanning login authentication, character progression, map instances, monster AI, skill processing, questing, and party/guild management. The codebase emulates the MapleStory client-server protocol for private-server developers and game-server architecture researchers. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__Rebirth.md)

Complements other MapleStory private-server stacks such as [[maplestory-v83-maplestory-cpp]], [[maplestory143]], [[rustms]], and [[mnwvs196]].
