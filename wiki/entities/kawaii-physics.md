# KawaiiPhysics

Simple fake-physics plugin for Unreal Engine 4 & 5 (C++). Centers on lightweight physics, animation, and asset-pipeline wiring for secondary motion (e.g. hair/cloth-style jiggle) without full PhysX simulation. Aimed at game developers, reverse engineers, and tooling builders in the Game Develop / Unreal source lane—not a cheat or anti-cheat artifact. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/pafuhana1213__KawaiiPhysics.md)

Complements same-author [[vtuber-with-ue4]] and other Unreal Game Develop surfaces ([[ue4-tutorials]], [[fightinggame-ue5]]) by focusing on AnimNode-style fake physics rather than full VTuber samples or general C++ tutorials.
