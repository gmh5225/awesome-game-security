# AndroidCheatTemplate

Two curated **Android cheat template** repos share this name:

**sanqiuu/AndroidCheatTemplate** — Android Unity cheat framework in C/C++ centered on OpenGL, Unity, and hooking—aimed at researchers in the cheat / game engine explorer:Unity lane. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/sanqiuu__AndroidCheatTemplate.md)

**gmh5225/AndroidCheatTemplate** — C/C++ Android cheat template emphasizing **kernel-level work**, **OpenGL**, and **memory analysis** for **Sausage Man** (cheat / game:sausage man). (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__AndroidCheatTemplate.md)

Complements GLES ImGui Unity mod-menu templates ([[imgui-unity]], [[imgui-unity-with-layout]]), Kotlin/Java floating-menu + JNI paths ([[android-mod-menu-kotlin]], [[android-modmenu-semijni]]), Android native ImGui apps ([[android-native-app-imgui]]), and kernel/driver scaffolds ([[android-kernel-driver-template]]).
