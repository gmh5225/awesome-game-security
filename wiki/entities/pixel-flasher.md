# PixelFlasher

Cross-platform GUI for flashing **Google Pixel** phones: bootloader unlock, factory and OTA image flashing, **Magisk** / **KernelSU** / **APatch** rooting, boot-image patching, and backup management. Distributed as a self-contained Python-built executable with no host runtime dependencies. Listed under Cheat / Android ROM tool for Pixel. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/badabing2005__PixelFlasher.md)

Sits in the Pixel bootloader/ROM/root lane beside boot-image tooling such as [[android-boot-image-editor]] and [[magiskboot]], root frameworks [[magisk]], [[kernelsu]], and [[apatch]], and Pixel-adjacent sources such as [[kernelsu-pixel4xl]] and [[easypixel]].
