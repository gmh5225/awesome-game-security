# Three.js

Comprehensive JavaScript 3D library: WebGL and WebGPU renderers, scene graph (cameras, lights, meshes), material and shader systems (PBR, TSL node-based shaders), loaders for glTF/FBX/OBJ, animation mixers, post-processing passes, physics integration, and XR support. De facto standard for browser-based 3D rendering, used extensively in web game development. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/mrdoob__three.js.md)

Sits in the Game Engine / HTML5 3D source lane—an OSS browser WebGL·WebGPU runtime for development and graphics study, rather than a cheat or anti-cheat artifact. Adjacent to [[engine]] (PlayCanvas), [[pixijs]] (2D WebGL/WebGPU), and [[turbulenz-engine]]; glTF/OBJ loader overlap with [[tinygltf]] and [[tinyobjloader]] on the asset-ingest side.
