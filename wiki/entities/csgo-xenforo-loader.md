# csgo-xenforo-loader

C++ **CS:GO cheat loader** (flowxrc) that authenticates users through a **XenForo forum backend**: **license verification**, **subscription-status checks**, **DLL downloading**, and **injection** via a loader app that gates access against a XenForo-powered website. Illustrates the typical **pay-to-cheat (P2C)** distribution model with **forum-based licensing**—useful for game-security researchers studying cheat distribution infrastructure and loader authentication mechanisms. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/flowxrc__csgo-xenforo-loader.md)

Sits beside PHP-auth CS:GO loaders such as [[csgo-loader]], commercial managed loaders such as [[cyber-ant-loader]], and manual-map inject research such as [[wizard-loader]].
