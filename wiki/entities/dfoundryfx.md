# DFoundryFX

**DFoundryFX** (DarknessFX/DFoundryFX) is an **Unreal Engine 5 plugin** that adds real-time **in-viewport performance tooling** for game projects. Implemented with **Unreal C++ modules**, it exposes customizable **metric charts**, **shader compilation monitoring**, and quick **STAT command** controls without requiring extra actors placed in a level. The plugin stays available directly during **gameplay and profiling sessions**, aimed at developers who need lightweight **runtime diagnostics** and **performance tuning** support—not cheat or anti-cheat tooling. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/DarknessFX__DFoundryFX.md)

Sits in the UE **Plugins:Unreal** performance-diagnostics lane beside cross-engine profilers such as [[optick]] and [[tracy]], and UE-specific tooling such as [[unreal-network-profiler]] and in-editor UI samples such as [[ue5-with-dear-imgui]].
