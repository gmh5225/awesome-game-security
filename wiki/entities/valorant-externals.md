# valorant-externals

Valorant **external** offset feed (README `[Offset]` tag) maintained for game security researchers and reverse engineers studying out-of-process offensive techniques in the cheat / game:valorant lane under [[vanguard]]. Offsets are refreshed incrementally over time rather than in one bulk dump. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__valorant-externals.md)

Sits beside [[valorant-dumper]] and the gmh5225 internal/GUI repos ([[valorant-internal]], [[valorant-internal-base]], [[valorant-gui-imgui-remake]]) in the per-title Valorant research lane, but scoped to external memory-layout offsets rather than in-process SDK generation or dump tooling alone.
