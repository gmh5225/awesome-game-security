# Fiw AntiCheat

Minecraft **server-side** mod verification and enforcement: challenges joining clients and evaluates companion-reported mod lists, versions, JAR fingerprints, and resource-pack state. Built in Java with Gradle for **Fabric**, **MinecraftForge**, and **NeoForge** across multiple game versions, centered on a **shared rule engine** with signature-based detection of unfair utility mods, blacklist or whitelist modpack modes, per-player profile history, and optional resource-pack auditing or bans.

Staff can roll out policy gradually with monitor-only logging, tiered player exemptions, admin commands for snapshots and rechecks, and integration hooks such as **Floodgate** for Bedrock players. Targets server owners who need practical client-mod visibility and modpack integrity rather than full behavioral or kernel-level anti-cheat. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Fi3w0__Fiw-Anticheat.md)
