# TurboDiff

IDA Pro plugin that discovers and analyzes differences between the functions of two binaries. Aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive static RE techniques in the cheat / IDA Plugins lane—comparing game client builds, anti-cheat modules, or patched binaries. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/helpsystems__turbodiff.md)

Complements other IDA-side binary diffing tools such as [[diaphora]] and curated packs like [[idaplugins]].
