# RIVER

**RIVER** (Runtime Inspector and Versatile Engine for Reversing) is Bitdefender’s **dynamic binary analysis framework** in the Cheat / DBI lane. It combines a custom **binary loader** (ELF and PE; external mapping; native import resolution) with **runtime instrumentation** for x86 executable analysis — aimed at binary-analysis researchers and malware analysts studying dynamic instrumentation and binary-loading frameworks. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/bitdefender__river.md)

Sits beside lightweight DBI peers ([[tinyinst]], [[cpp-veh-dbi]]) and loader/emulation stacks ([[pyda]], [[unicorn-pe]]) in the Windows/Linux RE instrumentation lane.
