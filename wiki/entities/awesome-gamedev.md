# awesome-gamedev

Curated list of free and open resources for game development (Calinou). Maintained in Markdown and organizes tools, engines, libraries, learning material, and assets across many languages and domains. Emphasizes openly licensed software and content, with additional notes on non-free options. Aimed at developers and researchers who need a broad reference index for discovering game-development tooling—not a cheat or anti-cheat artifact. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/Calinou__awesome-gamedev.md)
