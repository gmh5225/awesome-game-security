# liblava

Modern, easy-to-use C++23 library for Vulkan. Lean modular framework with rolling releases, aimed at low-level graphics programmers and performance-focused engine developers in the Vulkan/API space. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/liblava__liblava.md)

Sits in the Vulkan / Guide·API library lane—an approachable Vulkan foundation for engine and graphics research rather than a cheat or anti-cheat artifact.
