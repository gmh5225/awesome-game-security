# Simple EFT Base

Simple OOP cheat base for **Escape From Tarkov** (C/C++). Covers driver development, rendering, and animation in a title-specific scaffold aimed at game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive techniques in the cheat / game:eft lane. Offsets are most likely outdated and the author is not planning updates—useful mainly as a structural reference rather than a maintained stack. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/krispybyte__Simple-EFT-Base.md)

Same author as [[simple-rust-base]] (Facepunch Rust OOP base) and [[vook]] (VAC hook research). Complements discontinued Mono-era internal trainers such as [[escapefromtarkov-trainer]] for comparing EFT cheat scaffold patterns vs post-1.0 / [[il2cpp]] internal complexity.
