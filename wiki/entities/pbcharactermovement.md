# PBCharacterMovement

Unreal Engine character movement plugin that recreates classic Source and Half-Life 2–style FPS movement in UE4. Written in C++, it implements bunnyhopping, surfing, strafe boosting, wall strafing, and advanced crouch movement. Ships source and prebuilt binaries with setup instructions for integrating into Unreal projects—aimed at game developers who want fast, skill-based movement mechanics in modern FPS titles. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/ProjectBorealis__PBCharacterMovement.md)
