# BattleriteBot

**Battlerite arena brawler automation bot** (gmh5225). Reads game state through **memory access or screen recognition**, then automates gameplay actions including ability casting, targeting, and movement. Aimed at game automation researchers studying bot development for team arena combat titles. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__BattleriteBot.md)

Hybrid state-read paths (RPM vs visual/OCR) sit beside full-game automation samples such as [[tft-ocr-bot]], [[taskbarhero-bot]], and [[maplestory-worlds-automation]]; screen-recognition branches overlap zero-memory triggerbot lanes such as [[clickpic]] and [[camera-triggerbot]]. Defensive study pairs with [[ai-aimbot-detection]] and behavioral bot-detection research.
