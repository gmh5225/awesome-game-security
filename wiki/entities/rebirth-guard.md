# RebirthGuard

**RebirthGuard** (chztbby) is a **Windows C++ open-source anti-cheat library** listed under **Anti Cheat → Open Source Anti Cheat System**. Curated descriptions position it around **anti-cheat research**, **modding**, and **SDK generation**—aimed at anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers studying client protection integration rather than at cheat development.
