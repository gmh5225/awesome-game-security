# 3den Enhanced

**3den Enhanced** (R3voA3/3den-Enhanced) is a **feature expansion mod** for the **Arma 3 Eden Editor**. It is built from **SQF scripts**, **config definitions**, and **addon modules** that add **editor UI tools**, **mission attributes**, and **workflow shortcuts**. Scenarios can be played **without hard dependencies** on the editing extension—the mod targets mission makers and modders who want a more productive Arma 3 editing environment, not runtime cheat or anti-cheat research. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/R3voA3__3den-Enhanced.md)

Sits in the mission-editor / mod-authoring lane beside other Arma 3 tooling such as [[arma3-external-variable-manager]] (runtime variable manipulation) and UE4 editor automation samples such as [[automation-examples]].
