# ds4-tools

**DualShock 4 (DS4) controller tooling** (gmh5225; scripts for play and reverse-engineering on PC). Reads controller input and drives LED color, touchpad, motion sensors, and rumble motors over **HID protocol** communication—aimed at game developers and input-device researchers studying DS4 features on Windows. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__ds4-tools.md)

Sits in the peripheral **HID input** lane beside controller compatibility layers such as [[xidi]] and desktop input-simulation research under [[hardware-input-injection]].
