# godogen

Autonomous game-development stack for **Godot**, **Bevy**, and **Babylon.js** driven by **Claude Code** and **Codex**. Plans features, generates assets, and runs screenshot-guided self-repair loops organized along the engine axis—aimed at game developers, reverse engineers, and tooling builders wiring AI agents into multi-engine authoring rather than shipped-binary RE. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/htdt__godogen.md)

Sits in the **Game Develop → AI Agents** lane beside editor MCP bridges such as [[better-godot-mcp]] and agentic web-game stacks such as [[opengame]]—focused on prompt-to-playable iteration across Godot/Rust/JS engines with visual feedback loops.
