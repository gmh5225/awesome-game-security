# AntiCheat-detection

**AntiCheat-detection** (vipmaycom/AntiCheat-detection) collects **documented reverse-engineering notes** and **reconstructed reference implementations** for major commercial anti-cheat and DRM client stacks—including **FACEIT**, **Riot Vanguard**, **BattlEye**, **Easy Anti-Cheat**, **Denuvo**, and related usermode/kernel components. (source: wiki/sources/README-categories.md)

Listed under **Anti Cheat** / **Analysis Framework** in the README map. Treat write-ups and reconstructions as **research aids**, not ground truth for a specific shipped build—pair with [[research-rigor]] and version-scoped catalogs such as [[anti-cheat-observatory]].

## Scope (README summary)

- Multi-vendor AC client and driver behavior documentation
- Reconstructed implementations for study and cross-checking public claims
- Commercial stacks commonly referenced in PC competitive gaming security research
