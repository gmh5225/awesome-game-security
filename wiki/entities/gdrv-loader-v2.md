# gdrv-loader-v2

C/C++ project centered on loading and working with **`gdrv.sys`** — Gigabyte’s signed vulnerable driver commonly abused in [[byovd]] chains for kernel read/write and unsigned-driver mapping. Primarily driver development for game-security researchers and reverse engineers studying offensive cheat / vulnerable-driver techniques. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/holi4m__gdrv-loader-v2.md)

`gdrv.sys` appears in canonical LOLdriver inventories such as [[loldrivers]] and Microsoft’s vulnerable-driver blocklist lane ([[msft-driverblocklist]]).
