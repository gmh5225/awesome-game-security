# ExtendedCameraSettings

Game modification extending gameplay camera control beyond default engine limits. Provides adjustable field of view (FOV), camera distance, rotation freedom, and additional viewpoint parameters for enhanced gameplay or cinematic creation. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__ExtendedCameraSettings.md)

Useful for game-security researchers studying how client-side camera constraints are enforced and how modders bypass default engine camera bounds—complementary to title-specific FOV/freecam hooks such as [[cs2-fov-changer]] and [[ue4-freecam]].
