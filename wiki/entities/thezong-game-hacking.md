# Game-Hacking (TheZong)

Curated Markdown link list of game hacking and cracking learning resources: tutorials, forums, reverse-engineering references, and practical tooling such as dumpers and mappers. Includes starter bases and title-specific recommendations for CS:GO, GTA V, and Red Dead Redemption 2. Aimed at beginners and intermediate game-security researchers who need a fast on-ramp to study material and common tooling—not a runnable cheat or tool binary.
