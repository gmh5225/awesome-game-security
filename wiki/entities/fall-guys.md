# FallGuys

**FallGuys** is a cheat for **Fall Guys: Ultimate Knockout** (Unity) providing **movement** and **gameplay modifications**. It uses **kernel-mode driver communication** and **memory manipulation** to implement **speed hacks**, **fly hacks**, and other **physics-based exploits**. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/gmh5225__FallGuys.md)

Sits in the gmh5225 Fall Guys research lane beside KdMapper + ImGui kernel stacks such as [[flying-guys]] and [[flying-guys-fully-modified]], and the C# IL2CPP managed-injection alternative [[fall-guys-sharp]].
