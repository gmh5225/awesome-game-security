# d3dhook_imgui

C/C++ cross-API graphics hook sample for DirectX, OpenGL, and Vulkan Present/swap paths with Dear ImGui overlay wiring on x86 and x64. Aimed at graphics programmers and Windows game tooling developers building internal overlays or studying multi-API hook bootstrap in the DirectX / hook lane.
