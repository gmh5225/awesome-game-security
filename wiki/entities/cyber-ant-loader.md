# CyberAntLoader

Managed **cheat loader** (gmh5225) that wraps hack-DLL delivery in an authenticated pipeline: **HWID binding**, **license verification**, **anti-detection**, and **injection** support aimed at multiple anti-cheat-protected titles. Useful for game-security researchers studying commercial-style loader architecture—auth gating, device fingerprinting, and inject tradecraft bundled before feature modules run.
