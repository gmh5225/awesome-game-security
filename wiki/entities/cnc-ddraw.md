# cnc-ddraw

C/C++ **DirectDraw replacement wrapper** that improves compatibility for **legacy 2D games** on modern Windows and Wine-based platforms (Linux, macOS, Android). Adds pluggable rendering backends, shader-based upscaling, windowed and borderless modes, frame limiting, and quality-of-life hotkeys—aimed at retro players and modders who need stable rendering and modern display behavior in older titles such as classic RTS installs. Catalogued under Old Game / DirectX Compatibility. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/CnCNet__cnc-ddraw.md)
