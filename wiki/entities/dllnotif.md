# DllNotif

C++/C++ research sample focused on **`LdrRegisterDllNotification`** (Windows Ring3 DLL load/unload callbacks)—listed under DllNotification for anti-cheat engineers and defensive security researchers studying user-mode callback surfaces, manual mappers, and injectors that register or abuse DLL notification routines. Modding-oriented; complements in-debugger inspection via [[x64dbg-view-dll-notification]]. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/blaquee__dllnotif.md)
