# ProjectM

**On All Fronts** — a Unreal Engine 5 tactical FPS and RTS prototype focused on very large-scale combined AI and player battles. The codebase is primarily C++ with Unreal plugin content and uses the **Mass Entity** ECS framework to simulate tens of thousands of entities. Includes systems and examples for AI soldier control, command workflows, and large-map gameplay architecture. Primary audience: engine and gameplay programmers studying high-scale simulation patterns that can also inform multiplayer security and anti-abuse design. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/LeroyTechnologies__ProjectM.md)

Sits in the Game Engine / UE5 FPS Game lane — a large-scale Mass Entity simulation reference rather than a cheat or integrated anti-cheat product.
