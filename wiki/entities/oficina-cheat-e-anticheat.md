# Oficina Cheat e Anticheat

Portuguese **in-person workshop kit** for *The Invisible War: Cheats vs Anticheats* — a browser-based slide deck (HTML/CSS/vanilla JavaScript with a custom slide engine, interactive SVG architecture diagrams, and presenter notes) runnable locally without extra dependencies. Content walks from computer fundamentals through cheat categories (internal, external, kernel, and **DMA**) and anticheat layers (user-mode, kernel-mode, server-side), commercial systems, bypass patterns, and the attacker–defender adaptation cycle. Optional offline **Python demos** cover SHA-256 integrity checks and heuristic log analysis; supporting material includes a technical glossary, professor script, and curated analysis-tool references. Aimed at students, educators, and newcomers seeking a structured, **defensive-minded** overview rather than cheat deployment instructions. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/leviitin__oficina-cheat-e-anticheat.md)

Complements hands-on teaching samples such as [[basic-anti-cheat]] and kernel telemetry labs such as [[win-kernel-telemetry]].
