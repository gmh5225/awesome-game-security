MCAce is a privacy-first client visibility and admission stack for modern Minecraft networks that gives servers a narrow, consent-based view of a Fabric client and correlates it with independently produced server-side evidence. The project ships as a Fabric client mod plus proxy plugins for Velocity and BungeeCord and a backend plugin for Paper and Folia, written primarily in Java with a Gradle and Kotlin build targeting Minecraft 1.21.11, 26.1.2, and 26.2. After explicit per-connection user consent, it collects signed telemetry such as loaded mod lists, active resource packs, and shader selections, and ties client-reported facts to server anti-cheat signals through integrations like Grim and Vulcan. Evidence flows through Ed25519-signed frames with strict privacy boundaries, fail-closed policy evaluation, and bounded disposition actions such as observe, warn, challenge, and quarantine. It is aimed at server administrators who need auditable, reversible admission and anti-cheat evidence workflows rather than kernel-level or persistent client monitoring.
