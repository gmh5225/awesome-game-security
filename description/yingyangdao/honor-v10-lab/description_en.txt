This repository documents turning a locked Honor V10 (BKL-AL20) on EMUI 9 into a rooted, always-connected Android lab device for development, debugging, and traffic analysis. It captures the full unlock and root path—including software test point bootloader unlock without an official code—and how to work around Huawei-specific limits such as rigid SELinux and broken frida-server. The setup centers on Magisk 20.1, EdXposed, Frida Gadget injection, system CA trust for MITM, Termux tcpdump, and optional modules for root hiding and ad blocking. Supporting material includes step-by-step guides, pitfall notes, HwOUC reverse-engineering notes, reusable PowerShell and shell automation, and a harness skill for day-to-day device control. It is aimed at mobile security researchers, reverse engineers, and anyone who needs a reproducible Honor or Huawei Kirin test bed for hooking, packet capture, and app behavior analysis—including game clients and anti-tamper checks.
