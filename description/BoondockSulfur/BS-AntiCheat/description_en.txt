BSAntiCheat is a lightweight, false-positive-conscious anti-cheat plugin for Minecraft Paper 1.21.10+ servers, including full Folia support. Written in Java, it detects movement, combat, world-interaction, inventory, vehicle, and packet-level cheats such as speed, fly, reach, killaura, nuker, autoclicker, and x-ray mining. The plugin uses transaction-based lag compensation, configurable violation-level punishments with optional setbacks, SQLite logging, and integrations with PacketEvents, Discord webhooks, PlaceholderAPI, and LuckPerms. It is aimed at server administrators who want heuristic, tunable cheat detection that can be calibrated for their environment rather than flagging legitimate players by default.
