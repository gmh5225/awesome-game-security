AU-Anticheat is a defensive Among Us client plugin that adds anti-cheat validation and lobby moderation for hosts and players using BepInEx on the IL2CPP Windows build. It is implemented in C# for .NET 6 and hooks game networking to validate RPCs and packets, monitor movement and role behavior, and block or score suspicious actions before they are applied locally. Host-facing tools include configurable cheat detection with point-based kick or ban responses, minimum lobby level rules, saved lobby settings loadouts, chat filters, and local moderation lists keyed on friend codes. The project suits game security practitioners and community hosts who want client-side hardening against common Among Us exploits without remote telemetry, hardware fingerprinting, or third-party ban services.
