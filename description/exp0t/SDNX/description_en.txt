SDNX is a research port that statically recompiles the Xbox 360 retail build of Sleeping Dogs into native C++ and runs it on Nintendo Switch homebrew with a custom Vulkan graphics stack. The work centers on ReXGlue-based binary lifting from default.xex to AArch64, paired with a native renderer that interprets Xenos GPU command streams instead of emulating the original GPU. Shader microcode is translated ahead of time with XenosRecomp and DXC into HLSL and SPIR-V, while Python and C++ tooling parses archives, textures, shader containers, and automates bench and Switch builds. Primary languages are C++ and Python, with CMake-driven Switch and desktop targets using devkitA64, libnx, and Mesa NVK. It is aimed at reverse engineers and advanced porting researchers studying Xbox 360 executables, proprietary UE3-era assets, and low-level graphics replacement on constrained consoles.
