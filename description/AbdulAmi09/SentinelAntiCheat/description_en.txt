Sentinel Anti-Cheat is a chess integrity platform that analyzes games for statistical signs of engine assistance and produces explainable risk findings for human review rather than automated cheating verdicts. Built primarily in Python with a FastAPI backend and a Next.js arbiter dashboard, it combines a Regan-style move-quality baseline with seven signal layers covering complexity, timing, historical play, behavioral patterns, online behavior, and environmental or identity cues, fused into tiered risk scores with per-layer explanations. Stockfish-backed PGN analysis, Maia human-likeness comparison, optional XGBoost and Isolation Forest fusion, hash-chained audit logging, case management, live-game monitoring, and a partner API support federation-scale workflows. It is aimed at chess arbiters, chief arbiters, and federations investigating over-the-board and online integrity incidents.
