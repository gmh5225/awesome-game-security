GameSpice Anticheat is a Godot 4.7+ addon that provides server-side anti-cheat for dedicated multiplayer games through per-player suspicion scoring and client command sanity checks. It is implemented in GDScript as part of the GameSpice addon family and layers on top of GameSpice Server Security, turning speed, teleport, and aim-snap detections into decaying scores with configurable warn, kick, and ban thresholds without enforcing punishments itself. DotCommandSanity validates movement, look rates, ranges, and command rates on untrusted client input, while DotSuspicionWatch aggregates reports and emits escalation signals for the game or moderation stack to handle. The design is headless-server friendly, configurable via YAML and environment variables, and explicitly avoids client drivers or kernel hooks. It suits Godot developers who need a composable, server-authoritative anti-cheat scoring layer rather than a full client anti-tamper suite.
