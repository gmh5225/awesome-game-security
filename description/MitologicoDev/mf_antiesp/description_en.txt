FiveM AntiESP is a free, standalone client-side resource for GTA V FiveM servers that mitigates wallhack-style ESP by hiding other players from the local client when they are occluded behind geometry. It periodically scans on-screen player peds, uses native occlusion checks such as IsEntityOccluded and WouldEntityBeOccluded, and applies NetworkConcealPlayer so concealed players are not available for cheat overlays until they would legitimately be visible again. The implementation is written in Lua with a standard fxmanifest, requires no framework or server configuration, and exposes scan and reveal intervals through config.lua for tuning performance versus responsiveness. It targets server operators and anti-cheat workflows on roleplay or multiplayer hosts, though it is client-only and is meant to complement a full anticheat rather than replace it. Operators should test carefully because the concealment logic can conflict with other scripts that manage player visibility, instances, or routing buckets.
