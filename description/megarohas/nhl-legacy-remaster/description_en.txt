NHL Legacy Remaster is a Windows port and modernization project for EA Sports NHL Legacy Edition (Xbox 360), recompiling the original game to x86-64 with the ReXGlue SDK instead of emulating the console. The codebase is primarily C++ and CMake for the port, launcher, and runtime hooks, with a large Python toolchain for parsing EA archive formats, roster databases, uniforms, and Frostbite-era NHL 24 assets. It documents and implements reverse-engineering workflows such as manifest-driven codegen from default.xex, function hooking, save and franchise-data patching, and research utilities for binaries, textures, and game databases. The work targets developers and researchers who study legacy sports-game binaries, porting techniques, and client-side modding pipelines rather than online anti-cheat. End users combine legally owned Xbox 360 game files with built mods to play an updated franchise experience on PC with DirectX 12 or Vulkan.
