This is an educational guide and documentation wiki for understanding Counter-Strike 2 and Valve's Source 2 engine from the ground up, aimed at readers who may have no prior programming or reverse engineering experience. The Markdown-based curriculum covers computer fundamentals, PE and virtual memory concepts, Source 2 module architecture, offsets and signatures, the Schema and Entity systems, rendering, input and networking, VPK and KV3 resources, and static reverse engineering methodology. Content is organized into progressive learning paths and hands-on labs that rely on public dumps, demos, and practice binaries rather than live matchmaking processes. The site is published with MkDocs and Python, and the project deliberately excludes anti-cheat evasion or operational cheat development. It is intended for game security researchers, mod developers, and anyone studying CS2 internals through reproducible, evidence-labeled documentation.
