This is a living reference guide that tracks bootloader unlock, root, and jailbreak feasibility across major smartphone brands and chipsets, with emphasis on Chinese-market Android variants and iOS. Content is maintained as Markdown README snapshots and organized by vendor, covering unlock gates, version-specific fuse and anti-rollback behavior, unauthenticated Qualcomm 9008 EDL recovery, temporary root without unlocking the bootloader, efisp and fake re-lock workflows, and OEM restrictions such as built-in anti-cheat and tightened ADB policies. It is aimed at modders, reverse engineers, and security-minded users who need accurate, up-to-date device freedom status before upgrading firmware or pursuing root, kernel modules, or game-related tooling that depends on a controllable boot chain.
