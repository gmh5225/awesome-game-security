NovaGuard is a high-performance anti-cheat plugin for Paper 1.21 Minecraft servers. Written in Kotlin, it ships 44 individually toggleable checks that cover movement, combat, world exploits, macro-style automation, and x-ray mining, each with its own violation-level thresholds, punishments, and ban commands. Built-in staff tooling includes a suspects GUI, player freeze, ban waves, violation history, player reports, and Discord webhook alerts, while grace periods, lag shields, Bedrock exemptions, and VPN blocking help limit false positives. It is aimed at server administrators who need server-side cheat detection and moderation for multiplayer Minecraft worlds.
