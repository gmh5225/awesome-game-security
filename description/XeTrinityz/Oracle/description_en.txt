Oracle Trinity Edition is a modular in-game cheat and tweak menu for Batman: Arkham Knight on PC, opened with F3 or a controller and built as a BmSDK-AK mod. It is written primarily in C# as a ground-up rebuild of the original Oracle mod, with a small kernel, per-feature modules, guarded game hooks that log failures instead of crashing, and clean unload that restores changed values. The menu adds searchable pages, favourites on a radial wheel, combat automation including a trainable auto-fight brain, extensive teleport and world controls, a in-world editor, free camera and Batmobile tooling, save management, and optional custom music playback. It targets players and mod developers who want deep runtime control, experimentation, and quality-of-life cheats in a single-player Arkham Knight session rather than online anti-cheat or live-service tooling.
