JerryManager is a Magisk and KernelSU Android module that orchestrates Play Integrity bypass and root-detection evasion on rooted devices. It is built mainly from shell scripts with a browser-based Web UI, and integrates Play Integrity Fix, OhMyKeymint, Tricky Store, TEESimulator, and Zygisk Next to manage keyboxes, attestation, and per-app targeting. Key features include security patch and boot property spoofing, custom ROM fingerprint cleanup, banking mode that removes Frida and other hooking artifacts, and early-boot property injection to present a locked, non-debuggable device profile. It targets researchers and advanced users who need to conceal root, tampering, and instrumentation from apps and games that rely on Google Play Integrity and similar integrity checks.
