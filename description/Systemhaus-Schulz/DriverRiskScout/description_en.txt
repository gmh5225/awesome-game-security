DriverRiskScout is a read-only defensive tool for assessing Windows kernel-driver exposure and risk on endpoints. Written primarily in PowerShell with a Python-based central collector server, it inventories loaded, installed, and DriverStore drivers, verifies signatures, and correlates hashes against LOLDrivers, Microsoft's vulnerable-driver blocklist, and curated BYOVD research profiles. It applies static PE analysis, YARA rules, exploitability scoring, and event-log correlation from Sysmon and Code Integrity while detecting HVCI, VBS, WDAC, and other protection posture. Reports are exported as CSV, JSON, HTML, and Wazuh NDJSON, with optional NVD CVE enrichment and Sigma rule generation. It is aimed at defenders, incident responders, and game-security teams hunting BYOVD abuse and other kernel-level threats without exploiting drivers or modifying the host.
