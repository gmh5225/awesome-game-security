GameCube and Wii Game Fixes is a curated catalog of Dolphin-oriented patches, cheats, graphics fixes, Riivolution mods, and HD texture packs for retail GameCube and Wii titles. Each game is described in structured JSON entry files keyed by six-character disc IDs, and a Python build script validates those entries and emits machine-readable catalogs for apps or installers to consume. Entries cover Gecko, Action Replay, and on-frame codes, built-in Dolphin INI settings, and remote download metadata for third-party textures from GitHub, GameBanana, and other hosts without rehosting copyrighted assets. The project emphasizes proper author attribution, revision-specific codes, and emulation-focused improvements such as widescreen, framerate, and compatibility workarounds. It is aimed at preservation-minded players and reverse engineers who maintain or apply per-game patches inside Dolphin rather than at commercial anti-cheat or live online security.
