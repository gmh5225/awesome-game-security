PCILeechGen is a custom firmware generator for PCILeech FPGA DMA boards that clones the identity of a real PCI/PCIe donor device and produces a ready-to-flash bitstream. Written primarily in Go, it reads donor hardware through Linux VFIO and IOMMU, captures config space, BAR layouts, and capabilities, then generates SystemVerilog and COE artifacts for synthesis with Xilinx Vivado. It supports device emulation features such as NVMe controller behavior, MSI-X tables, config-space scrubbing, BAR profiling, MMIO tracing, and timing-oriented TLP latency tuning across many supported FPGA boards. The tool is aimed at security researchers studying PCIe DMA attacks, hardware identity spoofing, and anti-cheat or game-security defenses against memory-access cheat hardware.
