PCILeechGen is a custom firmware generator for PCILeech FPGA boards that reads a real PCI/PCIe donor device through Linux VFIO, clones its configuration space, BARs, and capabilities, and produces ready-to-flash FPGA bitstreams. Written primarily in Go with SystemVerilog firmware output, it automates scan, check, build, and validate workflows and integrates with Xilinx Vivado for synthesis. Key capabilities include config-space scrubbing, dynamic BAR emulation, NVMe admin-queue and DMA bridge support, MSI-X handling, and TLP latency and timing emulation for realistic device behavior. It supports many common PCILeech-compatible FPGA boards and can import offline MMIO traces to refine register behavior. The tool is aimed at security researchers studying PCIe and DMA attack surfaces, FPGA-based memory access techniques, and anti-cheat or hardware-trust research in educational settings.
