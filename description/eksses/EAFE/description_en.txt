EAFE (Autonomous Elytra Flight Engine) is a protocol-level navigation system for long-range autonomous elytra flight in Minecraft, pairing detailed architecture specifications with a Node.js mineflayer bot implementation. It models vanilla per-tick elytra physics—including axis-decoupled drag, pitch energy exchange, and firework boost cycles—through a finite-state machine that manages takeoff, cruise, descent, and precision landing. The design covers adaptive obstacle avoidance, Nether hazard scoring, player threat evasion, and fail-safe recovery for rubberbanding, chunk unload, and depleted resources. Anti-cheat evasion is woven into movement via cubic Bézier look-vector smoothing, Gaussian perturbation, slew-rate limits, and asymmetric packet jitter. It is aimed at researchers and developers studying Minecraft movement validation, bot navigation, and how automated clients mimic human-like flight under server-side anti-cheat scrutiny.
