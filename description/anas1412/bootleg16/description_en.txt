Bootleg16 is a from-scratch reimplementation of Counter-Strike 1.6 written in Rust on the Bevy engine, pairing classic GoldSrc movement, weapons, and economy with a modern 5v5 competitive flow. It loads original-style game data through dedicated GoldSrc asset readers and splits work across client, authoritative game server, and lobby crates with shared networking, simulation, and encrypted match traffic. Players get Steam authentication, voice chat, match recordings, parties and matchmaking, custom games with knife rounds and map vetoes, and deathmatch warmup. Server-side anti-cheat is built into the online stack, and the project ships Docker-based hosting for scaling lobby and game servers. It targets developers and operators studying multiplayer FPS architecture, legacy asset pipelines, and practical anti-cheat in a live competitive shooter.
