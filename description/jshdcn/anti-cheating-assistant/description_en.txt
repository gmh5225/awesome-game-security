Anti Cheating Assistant is an AI agent skill package that guides structured risk-control and anti-fraud work from incomplete business descriptions through modeling, analysis, and remediation. It is built around Markdown skill definitions and reference playbooks, with Python helpers for three-flow graph business modeling and for syncing an underground slang lexicon used in fraud investigations. Core capabilities include clarifying business logic, building three-flow diagrams of roles and carriers, mapping steps to a risk catalog, and recommending controls via domain playbooks covering account, mobile, transaction, marketing, content, and financial security. The bundled methodology and excerpts distill anti-cheating theory into layered knowledge meant for selective loading in coding assistants. It targets product owners, risk engineers, and anti-cheat or business-security practitioners who need a repeatable workflow to model online businesses and design proportionate defenses.
