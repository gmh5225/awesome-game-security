Iustitia is a client-side Fabric mod for Minecraft Java that passively detects cheating on other players by analyzing server-rebroadcast packets, with no server plugin or outbound reporting. It is implemented mainly in Kotlin on Gradle and uses Fabric mixins to observe combat and movement, covering legacy 1.8-style and modern 1.21-era hacks such as reach, kill aura, fly, speed, scaffold, and related combat anomalies. The mod ships many configurable checks with violation-level scoring, preset profiles for everyday play through strict moderation, in-game commands, HUD and nametag indicators, and replay tooling that reconstructs recent player motion as in-world ghost models for staff review. It is aimed at server moderators, anticheat researchers, and anyone studying Minecraft client-side game security who needs local inspection and evidence rather than automated kicks or bans.
