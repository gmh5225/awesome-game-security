Tag Kit for Steam is a Chromium and Firefox browser extension and Tampermonkey-style userscript that annotates Steam store listings with tags derived from Valve’s own store data and developer disclosures. Built around a shared TypeScript core with platform-specific adapters, it detects AI generated content reports, anti-cheat software (including kernel-level anti-cheat), third-party DRM, third-party account requirements, third-party EULAs, and PvP through Steam’s GetItems API with HTML fallbacks, then overlays badges, hover cards, and app-page banners across search, wishlist, hubs, and sale views. Per-tag settings let users show, dim, or hide labeled games, toggle banners, and maintain an ignore list, while the runtime stays content-script-only and avoids sending app data to third-party servers. It targets Steam shoppers and game-security-minded users who want at-a-glance visibility into anti-cheat posture, DRM, and related store policies before buying or installing a title.
