SourceRenderer is a toy game engine written in Rust with a custom low-level graphics stack, aiming to load and render Source-engine style game content alongside modern assets. It builds on large parts of Bevy for engine infrastructure while replacing windowing, asset management, and rendering with its own solutions. The graphics layer targets Vulkan 1.3 as the primary backend, with Metal and WebGPU support, and includes bindless resources, ray tracing where available, multi-draw indirect, occlusion queries, and pipelined multi-threaded rendering with PBR, SSAO, TAA, and related effects. An async asset manager can load glTF plus Source formats such as BSP maps, VPK packs, VMT/VTF materials, and MDL models, including CS:GO-related loaders. It is mainly useful for graphics and reverse-engineering work around Source engine maps and assets, and for experimenting with modern GPU-driven rendering across desktop, web, and mobile platforms.
