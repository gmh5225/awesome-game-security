Detect-Any is an open-source Android environment integrity framework that scans devices for compromise and tampering, then produces a weighted risk score with replayable evidence. It is implemented primarily in Kotlin as a multi-module Gradle project with JNI-backed native probes, a parallel collector pipeline, JSON rules, and a Jetpack Compose app for quick, full, and deep scan tiers. Coverage spans root and hook stacks (Magisk, KernelSU, Zygisk, LSPosed), attestation spoofing, emulators and cloud phones, dynamic instrumentation such as Frida, and behavioral signals including VPN, overlays, and screen sharing, backed by a hot-updatable Ed25519-signed signature database. Confidence grading and parallel orchestration aim to balance auditability and low false-positive rates for security engineers, mobile anti-fraud teams, and game or app integrity research on Android.
