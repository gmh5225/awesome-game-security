RektAntiCheat is a Java-based Minecraft server plugin that detects and blocks common client cheats and unauthorized player behavior. It ships as a Maven-built Spigot/Bukkit JAR and integrates PacketEvents with Netty-style packet interception to inspect network traffic alongside in-game movement and combat state. Detection modules cover combat abuses such as kill aura, reach, and autoclicker, movement hacks including fly, speed, phase, and scaffold, plus packet, inventory, timer, and crash-related checks. Optional join-time tooling can flag proxies and related connection metadata. Server operators and developers use it to harden multiplayer survival and PvP environments against widespread Minecraft hack clients.
