Go Hacking is a free, step-by-step tutorial and sample-code collection for reverse engineering Golang programs from scratch. It walks through foundational topics such as hello-world programs, primitive types, and control flow, with paired chapters for writing, debugging, and patching binaries on x64, ARM64, and ARM32. Each lesson includes Go source examples, companion C samples for comparison, and IDA Pro database files for hands-on analysis. The course is paired with a downloadable PDF book and targets security researchers, reverse engineers, and anyone learning to analyze Go executables at the assembly level.
