Wardogs anti-cheat is a server-side anti-cheat stack that ingests kill and player events forwarded from the Nightwatch game-server panel and enforces cheat detection and moderation actions in real time. It is implemented primarily in TypeScript as four cooperating Cloudflare Workers (event hook, scheduled cron, admin web tools, and faction-switch guard) backed by a shared Cloudflare D1 SQLite database. Detection centers on a karma scoring model with configurable rules for suspicious statistics such as extreme K/D, headshot rates, long-range kills, and frequent renames, plus optional Steam account risk checks including VAC and game ban history. The system integrates with Nightwatch for bans, kicks, announcements, whispers, and team changes, and ships operational extras such as auto-balance, clan friend checks, dashboards, and match economy leaderboards. It is aimed at operators of community or private game servers who want automated cheat heuristics and Steam-linked enforcement without running a traditional always-on backend.
