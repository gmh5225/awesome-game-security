VyxAC is a Minecraft server anti-cheat plugin for Spigot, Paper, and Purpur that emphasizes low false-positive bans through review-first workflows and corroborated evidence. It is written in Java (JDK 17+, built with Maven) and ships roughly forty-seven Bukkit event–based checks spanning combat, movement, inventory and world interaction, and coarse packet or timing heuristics. A lightweight statistical “mini AI” scores click rhythm, aim, and reaction patterns against the server’s clean-player baseline with a strict CPU time budget, while punishment logic can require multiple checks, ping and TPS guards, and grace periods during teleports, lag, and vehicles. Operators configure checks via YAML and in-game commands, including a customize GUI, violation profiles, and optional staff review buttons before kicks or bans. It targets multiplayer Minecraft administrators and game-security practitioners who want a self-hosted, highly tunable anti-cheat without relying on external packet-hook libraries by default.
