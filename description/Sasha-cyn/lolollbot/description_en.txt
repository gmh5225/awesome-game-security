lolollbot is a Geode client mod for Geometry Dash that records and replays frame-accurate click macros in the style of classic xDBot tooling. It is implemented in C++ with CMake, hooking core game layers such as PlayLayer, PlayerObject, and the menu and input dispatchers to capture inputs during record mode and inject them during playback. The mod persists per-level macro data on disk, supports practice-mode checkpoint rewinds, and bundles a configurable overlay with cheats including noclip, speedhack, FPS and TPS overrides, and several player and level-effect bypasses. It is aimed at researchers and players studying rhythm-game automation, client-side modification, and how unofficial tooling interacts with in-game anticheat checks such as spike-based death validation.
