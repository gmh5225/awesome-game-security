PayloadLab is a small research toolkit for experimenting with in-memory shellcode obfuscation and loader pairs in a controlled red-team setting. Python scripts transform raw x86 shellcode from C-style byte dumps into encoded or encrypted forms, while matching C++ loaders embed those payloads, decode or decrypt them at runtime, and transfer execution to the reconstructed buffer. Supported techniques include single-byte XOR, RC4, ChaCha20 stream encryption, run-length encoding, and IPv4-style byte encoding reminiscent of IPfuscation. The project is aimed at security researchers studying how payloads are hidden, how loaders reconstruct them, and how similar patterns relate to malware analysis, detection engineering, and game anti-cheat or EDR evaluation in authorized lab environments.
