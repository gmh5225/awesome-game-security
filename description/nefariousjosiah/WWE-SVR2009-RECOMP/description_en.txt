This project recompiles the Xbox 360 version of WWE SmackDown vs. Raw 2009 into a native Windows, Linux, and Steam Deck executable that runs without a full-system emulator. It is built mainly in C++ with CMake, using the ReXGlue SDK (derived from Xenia) to translate the game’s PowerPC binary to x86-64 and a custom native renderer based on re:Blue and plume for Direct3D 12 or Vulkan. Xbox 360 Xenos shaders are converted ahead of time with XenosRecomp, and the codebase includes hooks, pipeline caching, and optional developer tooling such as D3D call census and shader dumping for renderer reverse engineering. The intended audience is developers and researchers studying Xbox 360 recompilation, GPU translation, and preservation of legacy titles, as well as players who supply their own disc image to run the port legally.
