UniStory is a Unity-based MapleStory client reimplementation that loads official MapleStory WZ asset archives and renders maps, characters, and related game content in a modern engine. Written in C#, it relies on WzComparerR2.WzLib to parse KMS/TMS-format WZ data and implements MapleStory-style map layers, foothold physics, portals, sprite animations, and BGM playback from extracted assets. The project integrates Unity 2D rendering, NAudio for audio decoding, and LZ4-compressed WZ support through a modular loader for Character, Map, Mob, Npc, Skill, and other standard WZ categories. It is aimed at developers building private servers, studying MapleStory client mechanics, or reverse engineering and analyzing game assets from WZ files.
