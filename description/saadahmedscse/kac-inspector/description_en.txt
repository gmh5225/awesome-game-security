KAC Inspector is a Windows command-line tool that scans for kernel-mode anti-cheat drivers and reports whether each one is actively loaded, idle, or leftover on disk. Written in Rust, it correlates data from the kernel module list, the Service Control Manager, and the system driver store against a built-in database of dozens of known anti-cheat signatures, with heuristics for unlisted drivers that identify themselves as anti-cheat. The tool produces a concise verdict and per-driver status labels such as running, loaded, stopped, orphaned, or stale, including related user-mode services, without installing software, modifying the system, or using the network. It is aimed at players, researchers, and security-minded users who need a read-only view of kernel anti-cheat presence for troubleshooting, privacy assessment, or game-security analysis.
