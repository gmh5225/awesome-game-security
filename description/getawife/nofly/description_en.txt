NoFly is a fly-hack detection plugin for Paper 1.21.x Minecraft servers that combines synchronous player movement analysis with PacketEvents packet sanity checks. Written in Java, it tracks vertical and horizontal air movement, simulates expected gravity, and uses configurable buffers and leniency to flag hovering, sustained ascent, and glide-like cheating while exempting legitimate states such as creative mode, vehicles, elytra, and potion effects. When violations accumulate, the plugin can alert staff, log flags to disk, and apply setbacks that rubber-band players to their last valid position. It is aimed at server administrators who need lightweight server-side anti-cheat focused on detecting unauthorized flight rather than general exploit prevention.
