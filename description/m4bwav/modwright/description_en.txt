modwright is a Claude Code plugin and Python toolkit for modding PC games in a controlled, verifiable way. Its standard-library mw.py script scans game installs to detect the engine and runtime (Unity Mono or IL2CPP, Unreal, Godot, GameMaker, Source, and others), installed mod loaders, and anti-cheat markers such as Easy Anti-Cheat, BattlEye, and Vanguard, halting with a dedicated exit code when anti-cheat is present rather than suggesting injection or online cheats. It backs up saves and targeted files with SHA-256 manifests for restore and verification, validates that mods actually loaded by parsing BepInEx, MelonLoader, UE4SS, and related loader logs, and can prepare Steam Workshop VDF or Thunderstore packaging metadata without uploading. The workflow emphasizes decompiling before naming patch targets, offline or single-player modding, and repeatable automation for mod authors and security-conscious players who need engine and anti-cheat awareness during legitimate mod development.
