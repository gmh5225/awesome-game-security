WepAC is a hybrid anti-cheat plugin for Minecraft Spigot and Paper servers that combines rule-based violation checks with a self-learning neural network trained entirely in Java. It inspects player traffic through an embedded Netty packet handler, simulates movement physics to catch subtle fly and speed cheats, and runs a broad set of combat, movement, block, and inventory detections including KillAura, reach, auto-clicker, scaffold, and x-ray style behavior. The small MLP scores cheat probability per player, adapts check thresholds individually, learns online from punishments, staff labels, and filtered legitimate PvP sessions, and persists model weights across restarts. It also flags suspicious client brands from the Minecraft channel handshake and supports staff alerts, recordings, and configurable punishments. The project targets server operators and anti-cheat researchers who want adaptive, server-local machine learning on top of deterministic game-security checks.
