gh-remake is a structured game-hacking and reverse-engineering curriculum that repackages a large catalog of tutorial material into a Hebrew lesson site, short English lesson videos, and a longer “Game Hacking from Scratch” episode series built from live captures. Python drives catalog generation and video builds, while lessons weave together Cheat Engine memory work, small C++ demos, and HyperFrames rendering with Azure Speech narration. The curriculum spans foundations through memory scanning, IDA and Ghidra style tooling, injection and hooking, rendering overlays, kernel and anti-cheat internals, and related crackme and malware topics organized into many learning tracks. Offline single-player and open-source practice targets support hands-on labs for students and practitioners studying game security, anti-cheat mechanics, and reverse engineering.
