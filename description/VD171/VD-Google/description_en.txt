VD Google is a rooted Android application and optional flashable WebUI module that inspects what Google records locally about RCS messaging, Google Wallet, DroidGuard, and Play Integrity without using the network. It is built primarily in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and Material 3, uses superuser access to read system-protected databases and caches, and ships a bundled sqlite3 helper for on-device queries. The tool surfaces RCS and Wallet attestation state, Play Protect certification, cached integrity attestations with expiry times, and which apps recently requested Play Integrity checks, plus plain-language explanations and JSON export for automated device review. It declares no permissions (including no internet), targets KernelSU, APatch, and Magisk environments, and is aimed at security researchers, privacy-focused power users, and anyone analyzing mobile attestation and anti-abuse signals on Android.
