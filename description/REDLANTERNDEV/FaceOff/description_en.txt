FaceOff is a Windows utility that lets you disable FACEIT's kernel anti-cheat when you are not playing and re-enable it before your next match. It is built in C# as a WPF desktop app with a small CLI, and it toggles the FACEIT kernel driver, the FACEITService Windows service, and related client processes while persisting original settings locally. Because FACEIT only accepts a driver loaded at boot, the tool guides you through administrator elevation and restart when turning protection on or off. Batch scripts provide the same behavior without the executable. It is aimed at competitive FPS players and security-conscious users who want to limit always-on kernel anti-cheat exposure without uninstalling FACEIT.
