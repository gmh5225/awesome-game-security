Apple Silicon Port Factory is an experimental research framework and runtime that loads user-supplied Windows x86-64 PE executables and DLLs and runs them natively on Apple Silicon macOS and iOS without Wine, CrossOver, or D3DMetal at runtime. It is implemented primarily in Rust and combines an iced-x86-based x86-64 interpreter, block JIT, and disk-backed AOT cache with a clean-room Win32 layer, Direct3D 11/12 and Vulkan graphics routed through Metal 4 and MetalFX, and DXIL-to-Metal shader tooling. A factory CLI and ops database support PE scanning, Win32 import and instruction coverage audits, runtime hazard checks, anti-cheat compatibility reporting, offline AOT prewarming, and differential x86 verification for porting workflows. The monorepo targets developers and researchers who want to analyze, audit, and experiment with bringing legitimately owned Windows games to Apple Silicon, with optional per-game manifests and deep targets such as Overwatch and Alan Wake 2.
